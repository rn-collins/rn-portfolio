What this is

A strategic audit of your creator and brand system—not a social-media cleanup.

Most creator marketing audits stop at follower counts, engagement rates, and whether a campaign “felt good.” That is not enough if your brand depends on trust, expertise, regulation, or claims discipline. This audit answers deeper questions:

  • Are we choosing the right creators for the job the brand actually needs done?
  • Is our creator strategy aligned with our positioning, audience, and proof architecture?
  • Are our partnerships creating trust—or quietly eroding it?
  • Are we exposing the business to disclosure, testimonial, affiliate, or claims risk?
  • Are we measuring the right outcomes, or just reporting vanity metrics?
  • Do we have a system for governing creator content as the business grows?

What I audit

Six core layers of your creator ecosystem.

Layer 1

Brand + Positioning

Does creator activity reinforce or dilute the brand? Positioning, messaging hierarchy, proof architecture, offer clarity, audience alignment.

Layer 2

Creator Strategy & Fit

Selection logic, creator–brand fit, audience & platform fit, commercialization patterns—awareness vs education vs trust transfer vs conversion.

Layer 3

Trust + Audience

Authority signals, authenticity risks, audience vulnerability, educational vs promotional balance, testimonial and parasocial dynamics.

Layer 4

Disclosure, Claims + Risk

Sponsorship & affiliate disclosures, material connections, testimonial risk, claims language, substantiation gaps, AI/synthetic content.

Layer 5

Measurement

Are you measuring awareness, trust, conversion, risk, and learning—or just engagement? Conversion paths, affiliate tracking, audience quality.

Layer 6

Governance + Operating

Creator briefs, approval workflows, role clarity, claims review, AI-content guardrails, decision-making bottlenecks.

Engagement structure

Four steps.

  1. Intake + materials review. Campaign assets, creator lists, brand guidelines, disclosures, reporting, briefs, content examples.
  2. Audit + evidence synthesis. Review across all six layers; synthesize into a structured findings memo.
  3. Strategic recommendations. What to stop, fix, test, formalize, or scale.
  4. Debrief + roadmap. Decide the next step: internal implementation, a sprint, or governance support.

What makes this different from a typical influencer audit

Most influencer audits treat creators as a marketing tactic and stop at follower counts, content aesthetics, and one-off campaign performance. This audit treats creator activity as a brand, trust, governance, and business system—brand strategy, parasocial and credibility dynamics, disclosure and consumer-protection risk, platform realities, claims and substantiation, measurement quality, and long-term brand coherence.
