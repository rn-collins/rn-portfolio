# RN Builds — public professional framing

## Collection statement

100 Builds is a public demonstration of how Rayven-Nikkita (RN) Collins works across research, strategy, design and implementation. Each build starts with a consequential question, makes its assumptions and evidence legible, and turns the resulting argument into both a shareable film and an interactive web experience. The collection is less a showcase of isolated interfaces than a record of disciplined translation: from ambiguity to structure, from source material to decision, and from an idea to something another person can inspect and use.

## Polymath connection

RN works on questions too messy for a single discipline. Her practice connects law, neuroscience, developmental psychology, technology, culture and organizational design. “Polymath” here does not mean knowing everything; it means knowing how to bring multiple forms of evidence and expertise into one accountable process without flattening their differences.

## RN's role

Across the collection, RN acts as:

- Researcher: frames the question, gathers evidence and identifies uncertainty.
- Strategist: determines the audience, decision and useful form of intervention.
- Information and interaction designer: makes relationships, tradeoffs and next actions understandable.
- Builder: translates the concept into a working public prototype, film and supporting artifacts.
- Editor and evaluator: tests whether the result communicates truthfully and records its limitations.

## Method

1. Frame the consequential question and intended audience.
2. Separate verified evidence, inference, assumptions and unknowns.
3. Model the decision, system or experience that needs to become legible.
4. Build two complementary forms: a concise distributable argument and a deeper interactive artifact.
5. Test accessibility, technical behavior, comprehension and evidentiary boundaries.
6. Publish limitations and revise when the evidence or use case changes.

## Prototype and commissioned-work boundary

Unless a build expressly says otherwise, it is an independently initiated prototype—not commissioned work, an endorsement by a referenced organization, or a production system operating on live institutional data. Prototypes demonstrate a question, method and possible direction. Commissioned work begins with the partner's actual decision, evidence, constraints, users, governance and implementation environment.

## Work with RN

Bring RN a problem that crosses disciplines, audiences or institutional boundaries: an AI or evidence workflow, a complex public explanation, a decision instrument, a research-backed interactive, or a concept that needs to become demonstrable. A useful first conversation identifies the decision, who must trust or use the result, what evidence exists and what a bounded first engagement could deliver.

Primary call to action: **Discuss a project with RN**.
Secondary call to action: **View selected work**, for visitors who need a shorter curated portfolio before entering the full collection.

Do not promise a generic “free consultation.” The pathway should invite a serious project conversation and disclose the expected response channel and timing.
