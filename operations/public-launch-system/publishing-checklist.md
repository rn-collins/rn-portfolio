# Publishing checklist

## Before scheduling

- Watch the full 30-second MP4 with sound off and captions on.
- Confirm the poster, video, caption track and transcript belong to the same build.
- Read the LinkedIn post aloud; replace any phrase RN would not naturally say.
- Confirm the first two lines carry the hook before LinkedIn truncates the post.
- Confirm the claim is supported by the public build or clearly framed as a proposition.
- Open the production B-page anonymously and test video → motion story → interactive.
- Check the page title, social card, canonical URL and mobile layout.
- Add descriptive alt text when LinkedIn provides the control.
- Make the CTA match the post's actual purpose: explore, discuss, share or respond.
- Obtain RN's explicit approval immediately before publishing.

## After publishing

- Record the public post URL and publication time in the tracker.
- Test the LinkedIn post and RN Builds link while signed out.
- Respond to substantive comments in RN's voice; do not automate personal replies.
- Capture 24-hour and 7-day reach, dwell/video completion, saves, comments and qualified conversations.
- Log what the audience misunderstood or wanted next.
- Mark any newsletter, X, Instagram, carousel or talk reuse.

## Quality gates

A post does not ship when the video fails to decode, captions disagree with the frames, the first two lines are generic, a referenced claim lacks a defensible boundary, the build page is broken, or RN has not approved publication.
