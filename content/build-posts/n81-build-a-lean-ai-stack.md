## LINKEDIN TEXT POST

You don't need 12 AI tools.

You need 3. Maybe 4. Here's the framework.

AI tool FOMO is expensive — not just in subscriptions but in the mental overhead of managing tools you barely use. Every week something new drops. You try it. It sits in your browser tabs. You still just open ChatGPT.

The fix isn't a better tool. It's a stack.

What I built:
**Build a Lean AI Stack** — a free framework guide inside Aloha AI University.

Four layers of a working AI stack:
→ **Writing/thinking layer** — your primary AI model (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini — pick one and go deep)
→ **Research layer** — a tool with web access and citations (Perplexity, Claude with search)
→ **Automation layer** — one tool that connects your apps (Zapier, Make, or nothing if you don't need it yet)
→ **Specialized layer** — one domain-specific tool for your actual workflow (content, meeting notes, code, etc.)

Plus: a cut-or-keep audit framework for what you're paying for right now.
