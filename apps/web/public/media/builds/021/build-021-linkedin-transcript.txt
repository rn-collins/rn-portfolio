BUILD 021 — Break the Promise Open

SCENE 1
BUILD 021. THE CLAIM SOUNDS CERTAIN. Evidence may not support it.

SCENE 2
SEPARATE. PROMISE ≠ EVIDENCE. Store claim and proof separately.

SCENE 3
TEST. SCOPE. DATE. METHOD. OWNER. Does it cover this use?

SCENE 4
CONTROL. NAME THE FAILURE PATH. Owner, trigger, response, retained record.

SCENE 5
VERDICT. SUPPORTED. PARTIAL. UNVERIFIED. CONTRADICTED. Preserve uncertainty and next action.

SCENE 6
PROCUREMENT. BUY THE EVIDENCE. Keep claim, proof, control, recourse inspectable.

FINAL PROPOSITION
Buy the evidence—not the adjective.
