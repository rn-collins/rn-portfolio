SCENE 1
BUILD 001. HUMAN IN THE LOOP?. That phrase hides the part that matters.

SCENE 2
ASK 01. WHEN DO THEY SEE IT?. Before the decision, while it can still change—not after the consequence.

SCENE 3
ASK 02. WHAT CAN THEY SEE?. Evidence, uncertainty, alternatives and what the system actually did.

SCENE 4
ASK 03. WHAT CAN THEY DO?. Approve is not enough. Change, stop, escalate and record must be real powers.

SCENE 5
THE ARCHITECTURE. TIMING · EVIDENCE · AUTHORITY · ESCALATION · RECORD. Human presence becomes meaningful only when these controls work together.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. DESIGN THE REVIEW, NOT THE LABEL.. Test whether the reviewer can actually change or stop the outcome.

FINAL PROPOSITION
A HUMAN IS A CONTROL ONLY IF THEY CAN CHANGE THE OUTCOME.
