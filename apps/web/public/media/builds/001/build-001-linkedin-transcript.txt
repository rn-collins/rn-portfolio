BUILD 001 — Human Review Design Framework

SCENE 1
BUILD 001. HUMAN IN THE LOOP? That label hides what matters.

SCENE 2
TIMING. WHEN DO THEY SEE IT? Before the consequence, while change remains possible.

SCENE 3
EVIDENCE. WHAT CAN THEY SEE? Show uncertainty, alternatives, and system behavior.

SCENE 4
AUTHORITY. WHAT CAN THEY DO? Change, stop, escalate, and record.

SCENE 5
ARCHITECTURE. CONTROLS MUST WORK TOGETHER. Timing. Evidence. Authority. Escalation. Record.

SCENE 6
REVIEW. DESIGN THE POWER—NOT THE LABEL. Human presence alone changes nothing.

FINAL PROPOSITION
A human is a control only if they can change the outcome.
