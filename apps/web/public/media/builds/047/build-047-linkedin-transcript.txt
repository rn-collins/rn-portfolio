SCENE 1
BUILD 047. A GOOD IDEA DOES NOT ARRIVE CONTEXT-FREE. A policy, technology, service, or program can succeed somewhere else and still fail when assumptions about people, place, infrastructure, authority, and resources change.

SCENE 2
DEFINE THE INTERVENTION. PURPOSE · INPUT · WORKFORCE · COST. Use synthetic profiles to bind the intervention to its intended outcome, evidence, language, staffing, funding, technology, delivery model, dependencies, and nonnegotiable limits.

SCENE 3
MOVE IT BETWEEN PLACES. ACCESS · CAPACITY · CULTURE · INFRASTRUCTURE. Compare declared conditions without ranking communities. Expose missing workforce, connectivity, language, mobility, institutional support, local knowledge, and community authority.

SCENE 4
RECOMPUTE FIT. KEEP · ADAPT · BUILD CAPACITY · STOP. When the place changes, recompute every gap and required adaptation. Preserve uncertainty, evidence limits, affected people, tradeoffs, ownership, and conditions that block transfer.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. A SCORE IS NOT FEASIBILITY OR CONSENT. A synthetic match cannot establish effectiveness, legality, safety, funding, procurement readiness, community acceptance, implementation capacity, or permission to proceed.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. MAKE TRANSFERABILITY EARN ITS NAME. TRANSFERABILITY MUST BE EARNED BY TESTING THE INTERVENTION AGAINST THE PLACE, ITS PEOPLE, AND ITS DEPENDENCIES.
