BUILD 019 — The Alert That Knows Who Cares

SCENE 1
BUILD 019. 100 ALERTS ≠ 100 INCIDENTS. Repeated signals may describe one event.

SCENE 2
CORRELATE. GROUP EVIDENCE FIRST. Preserve each source and state.

SCENE 3
ASSESS. SCORE CONSEQUENCE TRANSPARENTLY. Keep severity, scale, rights, and reversibility visible.

SCENE 4
ROUTE. AUTHORITY BEFORE AVAILABILITY. Notify the right owner, with reasons.

SCENE 5
ACKNOWLEDGE. MISSED CLOCK → NAMED BACKUP. Record response or escalate.

SCENE 6
ESCALATION. CHAIN—NOT VOLUME. Authority, evidence, time, recourse.

FINAL PROPOSITION
Escalation is a chain of authority, evidence, time, and recourse.
