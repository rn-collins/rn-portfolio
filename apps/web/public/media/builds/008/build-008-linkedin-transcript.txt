BUILD 008 — Data Is Not a Decision

SCENE 1
BUILD 008. A WALL OF CHARTS. The decision is still missing.

SCENE 2
START. NAME THE DECISION. Every metric must affect it.

SCENE 3
EVIDENCE. SOURCE. FRESHNESS. COMPARISON. UNCERTAINTY. Context makes numbers legible.

SCENE 4
ACTION. DECISION → OWNER → NEXT STATE. Add trigger, deadline, authority, escalation.

SCENE 5
CONTROL. CONFLICTS NEED A PERSON. Dashboards expose; humans resolve.

SCENE 6
OUTCOME. DATA MUST ENABLE ACTION. Visibility alone is not usefulness.

FINAL PROPOSITION
Data becomes useful when someone can decide and act.
