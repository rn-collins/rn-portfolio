BUILD 052-B — Synthetic Cannabis Comparison Literacy

1. A NAME IS NOT A VERIFIED PRODUCT RECORD. A familiar label does not supply verified identity, composition, quality, effect or safety.
2. OPEN THE FICTIONAL RECORD. Compare declared name, format and relative bands while keeping missing evidence visible.
3. FIELDS ARE NOT FINDINGS. Changing a comparison reorganizes fields; it does not create equivalence or fit.
4. PRESERVE THE GAPS. No laboratory result, verified label, dose, batch, person or current law is represented.
5. COMPARE—DO NOT RECOMMEND. The demo cannot rank, match, predict, guide purchase or interpret a certificate.
6. LOOK BEYOND THE NAME. A responsible comparison begins by showing which evidence is still missing.

LIMITATION
Synthetic fields only. No physiological, product-quality, legal, safety, effect or purchase inference is produced.
