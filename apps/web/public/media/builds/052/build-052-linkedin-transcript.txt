SCENE 1
BUILD 052. A STRAIN NAME IS NOT A PRODUCT MODEL. The same or similar label can conceal different formats, declared composition, batches, evidence, contexts, and unknowns.

SCENE 2
OPEN THE COMPARISON. COMPOSITION · FORMAT · CONTEXT. Use exact-bound synthetic products and relative bands. Keep label, goal, constraints, missing evidence, and NOT COLLECTED person context visible.

SCENE 3
COLLAPSE THE LABEL. LET THE DIMENSIONS REORGANIZE. When the learning goal or comparison pair changes, reorganize only the visible dimensions. Do not turn similarity into equivalence, effect, quality, or fit.

SCENE 4
PRESERVE THE GAPS. DECLARED · SYNTHETIC · UNKNOWN. A displayed band is not a dose, potency result, laboratory analysis, verified label, batch record, predicted experience, or current legal status.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. COMPARE—DO NOT RECOMMEND. The demo cannot rank products, match one to a person, predict effects, guide purchase or use, interpret a certificate, or establish safety, legality, or effectiveness.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. LOOK BEYOND THE NAME. A NAME CANNOT CARRY COMPOSITION, FORMAT, CONTEXT, UNCERTAINTY, OR A PERSON'S GOALS.
