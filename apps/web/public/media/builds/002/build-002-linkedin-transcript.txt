BUILD 002 — Multi-Audience Meaning Architecture

SCENE 1
BUILD 002. ONE IDEA. MANY AUDIENCES. The factual core must survive.

SCENE 2
SOURCE TRUTH. LOCK WHAT CANNOT MOVE. Names, numbers, negation, uncertainty, material facts.

SCENE 3
TRANSFORM. CHANGE THE EXPLANATION. Adapt register, context, examples, and vocabulary.

SCENE 4
VERIFY. CHECK DRIFT SEPARATELY. Fluency does not prove fidelity.

SCENE 5
BOUNDARY. ANCHOR CHECKS ARE ALARMS. Consequential communication still needs competent review.

SCENE 6
ORDER. TRUTH → AUDIENCE → VERIFY. Never make the rewrite its own source.

FINAL PROPOSITION
Change the language. Keep the truth anchored.
