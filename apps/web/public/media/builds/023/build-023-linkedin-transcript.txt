BUILD 023-B — Consent & Data Rights Manager

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
Bundled consent. BUNDLED CONSENT HIDES THE DEAL. A single yes cannot show purpose, data, recipient, retention, reuse, or withdrawal.

SCENE 2
Specificity. SEPARATE EVERY PURPOSE. Each purpose needs its own scope, permission state, evidence, and decision history.

SCENE 3
Event truth. EVENTS MUST TELL THE TRUTH. Grant and withdrawal records must form a valid, chronological, append-only state chain.

SCENE 4
Withdrawal. WITHDRAWAL CHANGES WHAT HAPPENS NEXT. It stops future consent-based processing without erasing prior facts or overriding lawful obligations.

SCENE 5
Rights routes. ROUTE EVERY RIGHTS REQUEST. Access, correction, deletion, retention, withdrawal, and reuse need an owner, deadline, status, and escalation.

SCENE 6
Save this. CONTROL REQUIRES A LIVING RECORD. Consent is specific, changeable, evidenced, and never the only possible legal basis.

FINAL PROPOSITION
CONSENT IS SPECIFIC, CHANGEABLE, EVIDENCED, AND NEVER THE ONLY POSSIBLE LEGAL BASIS.
