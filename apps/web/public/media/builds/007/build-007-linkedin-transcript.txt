SCENE 1
BUILD 007. THE PRODUCT SHIPS.. But shipping is one-way. Collection alone does not make a product learn.

SCENE 2
CAPTURE. HEAR FOUR DIFFERENT SIGNALS.. USE · OUTCOME · CORRECTION · UNMET NEED

SCENE 3
QUALIFY + ROUTE. PURPOSE · CONSENT · CONSEQUENCE · OWNER. Urgent corrections enter human triage before the ordinary request queue.

SCENE 4
HUMAN CONTROL. EVIDENCE · AUTHORITY · CAPACITY · ESCALATION. A person in the loop matters only if they can pause, change, record and escalate.

SCENE 5
CLOSE THE LOOP. CHANGE → VALIDATE → COMMUNICATE → MONITOR. The product learns when a bounded improvement safely returns to observed outcomes.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. COLLECTION IS NOT LEARNING.. Design the accountable path by which feedback can responsibly change the product.

FINAL PROPOSITION
A PRODUCT LEARNS ONLY WHEN FEEDBACK CAN RESPONSIBLY CHANGE IT.
