BUILD 007 — The Product That Learns

SCENE 1
BUILD 007. SHIPPING IS ONE-WAY. Collection alone is not learning.

SCENE 2
CAPTURE. USE. OUTCOME. CORRECTION. NEED. Hear distinct signals.

SCENE 3
ROUTE. PURPOSE. CONSENT. CONSEQUENCE. OWNER. Urgent corrections enter human triage.

SCENE 4
CONTROL. CAN HUMANS CHANGE IT? They need evidence, authority, capacity, and escalation.

SCENE 5
LOOP. CHANGE → VALIDATE → COMMUNICATE → MONITOR. Return improvements to observed outcomes.

SCENE 6
LEARNING. FEEDBACK MUST CHANGE THE PRODUCT. Responsibly, visibly, and safely.

FINAL PROPOSITION
A product learns only when feedback can responsibly change it.
