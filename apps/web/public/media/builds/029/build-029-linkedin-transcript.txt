BUILD 029-B — Change & Consequence Explorer

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 029. EVERY CHANGE ENTERS A SYSTEM. A policy edit, model update, data field, or workflow shortcut can alter outcomes far beyond the place it began.

SCENE 2
BOUND THE CHANGE. NAME WHAT WILL BE DIFFERENT. Record the actor, object, purpose, timing, assumptions, dependencies, and the prior state being replaced.

SCENE 3
TRACE CONSEQUENCE. FOLLOW WHO AND WHAT IT CAN REACH. Map direct effects, downstream handoffs, delayed effects, feedback loops, rights, and people carrying the burden.

SCENE 4
TEST SCENARIOS. EXPECTED · EDGE · FAILURE · MISUSE. Compare evidence, uncertainty, reversibility, scale, severity, and the conditions that would change the conclusion.

SCENE 5
GOVERN THE MOVE. OWNER · MONITOR · STOP · REMEDY. A release needs accountable authority, observable signals, rollback criteria, notice, correction, and recourse.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. FOLLOW THE CONSEQUENCE PAST THE CHANGE. A CHANGE IS NOT SMALL WHEN ITS CONSEQUENCES TRAVEL FARTHER THAN ITS AUTHOR CAN SEE.

FINAL PROPOSITION
A CHANGE IS NOT SMALL WHEN ITS CONSEQUENCES TRAVEL FARTHER THAN ITS AUTHOR CAN SEE.
