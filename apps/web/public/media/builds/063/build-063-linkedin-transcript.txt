SCENE 1
BUILD 063. RESEARCH SHOULD NOT DIE IN A FOLDER. A folder can store files while questions, sources, claims, gaps, methods, changes, and outputs lose their relationships.

SCENE 2
REGISTER THE QUESTION. QUESTION · SOURCE · NOTE · CLAIM. Use exact-bound synthetic research objects. Preserve identifiers, source versions, check dates, locators, notes, claims, methods, gaps, updates, and dependent outputs.

SCENE 3
KEEP THE TRAIL ALIVE. GAP · CHANGE · REVIEW · OUTPUT. When a source grows stale, support disappears, a method changes, or an output ages, retain the relationship and expose the required review state.

SCENE 4
DO NOT COLLAPSE THE RECORD. CHECKED · LINKED · CURRENT · OPEN. Keep each label bounded to the fixed fixture. A repository state does not establish truth, authority, quality, validity, rights, or publication readiness.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. A REPOSITORY IS NOT PROOF. The demo cannot determine truth, authority, completeness, causation, scientific validity, legal duty, ownership, permission, or approval to publish.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. THE RESEARCH KEEPS MOVING. THE RESEARCH KEEPS MOVING WHEN QUESTIONS, SOURCES, CLAIMS, GAPS, METHODS, UPDATES, AND OUTPUTS REMAIN CONNECTED.
