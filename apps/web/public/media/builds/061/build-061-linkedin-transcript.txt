BUILD 061-B — Why Did We Decide That?

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 061. A DECISION RECORD CAN LOSE ITS REASONS. The final choice may survive while evidence, assumptions, alternatives, dissent, uncertainty, authority, expected outcomes, and later learning disappear.

SCENE 2
REBUILD THE THEN RECORD. EVIDENCE · ASSUMPTION · ALTERNATIVE · DISSENT. Use an exact-bound synthetic decision. Preserve what was recorded as available, who asserted it, versions, dates, unknowns, constraints, options, objections, approvals, and expected outcomes.

SCENE 3
OPEN THE TIME MACHINE. KNOWN THEN · KNOWN NOW · HINDSIGHT. Keep later evidence and outcomes outside the original decision frame. Compare timelines without rewriting what the record shows was known or claimed then.

SCENE 4
TRACE WHAT CHANGED. SOURCE · ASSUMPTION · OUTCOME · INTERPRETATION. When a source, assumption, alternative, dissent note, or observed outcome changes, update only declared links and preserve contradictions, gaps, and unresolved attribution.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. RECOVERY IS NOT JUDGMENT. The demo cannot establish truth, causation, reasonableness, authority, completeness, correctness, legality, negligence, intent, accountability, or who caused an outcome.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. SEPARATE THEN FROM NOW. A DECISION BECOMES REVIEWABLE WHEN ITS EVIDENCE, ASSUMPTIONS, ALTERNATIVES, DISSENT, AUTHORITY, EXPECTATIONS, OUTCOMES, AND HINDSIGHT STAY DISTINCT.

FINAL PROPOSITION
