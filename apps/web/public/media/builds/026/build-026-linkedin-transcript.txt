SCENE 1
BUILD 026. A CLEAN TABLE CAN HIDE A MESSY HISTORY. Completeness on screen does not prove the records are accurate, representative, current, or fit for this decision.

SCENE 2
PROVENANCE. TRACE WHERE EVERY FIELD CAME FROM. Keep source, collection method, transformation, timestamp, owner, and permitted use inspectable.

SCENE 3
QUALITY. TEST THE FAILURE MODES. Missingness, duplication, conflict, drift, outliers, and label uncertainty must remain separate signals.

SCENE 4
REPRESENTATION. ASK WHO IS MISSING. Coverage can look large while systematically excluding people, places, outcomes, or hard-to-record cases.

SCENE 5
DECISION FITNESS. BOUND THE CLAIM AND THE USE. Name what the data can support, what it cannot support, the accountable reviewer, and the next verification action.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. DO NOT CLEAN AWAY THE WARNING. MESSY DATA CAN SUPPORT A DECISION ONLY WHEN ITS LIMITS REMAIN VISIBLE.
