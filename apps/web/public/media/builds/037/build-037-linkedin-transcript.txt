BUILD 037-B — Feedback → Change Governance Lab

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 037. FEEDBACK IS A SIGNAL—NOT A PRODUCT COMMAND. A comment can reveal correction, outcome, unmet need, misuse, access barrier, or preference without proving what should change.

SCENE 2
CAPTURE SAFELY. PURPOSE · CONSENT · MINIMIZATION · PROVENANCE. Use synthetic reporting in the lab. Separate content from identity, protect sensitive detail, preserve context, and offer correction and withdrawal routes.

SCENE 3
QUALIFY THE SIGNAL. EVIDENCE · RECURRENCE · CONSEQUENCE · UNCERTAINTY. Deduplicate carefully, keep minority and contradictory reports visible, test source limits, and route urgent harm to accountable human triage.

SCENE 4
GOVERN THE CHANGE. PROPOSAL · OWNER · AUTHORITY · DECISION. Link the feedback to a bounded change, alternatives, affected people, evidence, risks, dependencies, approval, dissent, conditions, and rollback.

SCENE 5
CLOSE THE LOOP. VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE · MONITOR · CORRECT. Test whether the change works, tell reporters what happened when appropriate, watch intended and unintended outcomes, and preserve the record.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. SIGNAL → DECISION → VERIFIED CHANGE. FEEDBACK BECOMES LEARNING ONLY THROUGH AN ACCOUNTABLE PATH FROM SIGNAL TO VERIFIED CHANGE.

FINAL PROPOSITION
FEEDBACK BECOMES LEARNING ONLY THROUGH AN ACCOUNTABLE PATH FROM SIGNAL TO VERIFIED CHANGE.
