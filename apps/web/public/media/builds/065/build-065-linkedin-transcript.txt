BUILD 065-B — Fictional Psychedelic Pathway Record

1. ONE EVENT DOES NOT SHOW THE WHOLE RECORD. A fictional session can sit inside a longer modeled pathway without defining care.
2. EXPAND THE FIXTURE. Connect declared purpose, permission, handoff, follow-up, evidence and recourse states.
3. KEEP STATES DISTINCT. Unaccepted, withdrawn and overdue remain separate fictional states with no legal effect.
4. A RECORD IS NOT A LIFELINE. The fixture does not monitor, diagnose, triage, treat or provide emergency response.
5. MAKE THE FICTIONAL RULES VISIBLE. Owners and transitions belong to this model—not a clinical or legal standard.
6. KEEP STATE AND LIMIT TOGETHER. A pathway record is legible when each declared rule and unknown remains visible.

LIMITATION
Fictional fixture only. It cannot deliver care, verify consent or providers, allocate duty, guarantee follow-through, determine safety, or replace emergency and crisis services.
