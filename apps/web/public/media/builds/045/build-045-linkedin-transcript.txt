SCENE 1
BUILD 045. AVAILABILITY IS NOT ACCESS. A program or product can exist while a person still cannot understand the rules, qualify, afford the process, reach care, find supply, or continue safely.

SCENE 2
MAP THE JOURNEY. LEARN · QUALIFY · PAY · TRAVEL. Use synthetic people and places to trace required information, eligibility, documents, fees, appointments, distance, time, mobility, language, privacy, and support.

SCENE 3
TEST THE CARE PATH. CLINICIAN · PRODUCT · SUPPLY · FOLLOW-UP. Keep clinical judgment human. Show availability, product and formulation constraints, pharmacy or dispensary steps, continuity, monitoring, and what remains unknown.

SCENE 4
CHANGE THE CONTEXT. COST · MOBILITY · ELIGIBILITY · LOCATION. When one constraint changes, reroute the declared path and recompute time, expense, travel, required steps, barriers, alternatives, and unresolved dead ends.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE CLAIM. A ROUTE IS NOT CARE OR LEGAL ADVICE. A modeled journey cannot verify current law, eligibility, clinical appropriateness, product safety, inventory, affordability, accessibility, or a successful outcome.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. MEASURE THE WHOLE JOURNEY. ACCESS IS THE WHOLE JOURNEY THROUGH INFORMATION, ELIGIBILITY, COST, TRAVEL, CLINICIANS, SUPPLY, AND CONTINUING CARE.
