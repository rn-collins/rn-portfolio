BUILD 067-B — A Digital Twin Should Know What It Does Not Own

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 067. A DIGITAL TWIN BEGINS WITH GAPS. Fixed synthetic knowledge objects begin with missing context, undeclared ownership, blocked inference, and permission that may be withheld.

SCENE 2
FORM A BOUNDED MODEL. OBJECTS CONNECT ONLY THROUGH EXPLICIT CONTEXT. Keep knowledge, preferences, values, roles, relationships, permissions, context, sources, and declared use limits distinct.

SCENE 3
SEPARATE THE STATES. DECLARED IS NOT INFERRED. Similarity is not authority. Possession is not ownership. A model entry is not consent, identity, truth, or permission to act.

SCENE 4
PRESERVE THE HOLDS. WITHHELD · EXPIRED · UNDECLARED · BLOCKED. Do not fill gaps by inventing permission, current context, ownership, personal traits, motives, diagnoses, or psychological profiles.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. A MODEL OF BOUNDARIES, NOT A CLAIM OF RIGHTS. This fixed synthetic demonstration stores no personal data, verifies no identity, and does not persist, monitor, score, or surveil.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. UNKNOWN IS A VALID STATE. A PERSONAL MODEL IS USEFUL WHEN PERMISSION, SOURCE, DECLARED OR INFERRED STATE, CONTEXT, OWNERSHIP STATUS, AND LIMITS STAY CONNECTED.

FINAL PROPOSITION
UNKNOWN IS A VALID STATE.
