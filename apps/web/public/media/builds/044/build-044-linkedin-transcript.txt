BUILD 044-B — Reuse the Parts That Regulation Keeps Requiring

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 044. RECURRING REQUIREMENTS ARE NOT COPY-PASTE BLOCKS. Approvals, consent, evidence, disclosures, and records recur, but the rule, actor, purpose, jurisdiction, risk, and effective date can change what is valid.

SCENE 2
DEFINE THE COMPONENT. PURPOSE · INPUT · OUTPUT · OWNER. Use synthetic records to bind each component to authority, evidence, required fields, accountable role, review gate, version, effective period, and prohibited reuse.

SCENE 3
ASSEMBLE THE WORKFLOW. APPROVE · CONSENT · DISCLOSE · RECORD. Compose only compatible components. Preserve order, dependencies, decisions, signatures, provenance, retention, correction, withdrawal, and human review.

SCENE 4
CHANGE THE CONTEXT. ACTOR · USE · PLACE · CONSEQUENCE. When a declared condition changes, recompute applicability, required evidence, permissions, language, review, downstream records, and every affected component.

SCENE 5
INVALIDATE EXPLICITLY. STALE · CONFLICT · MISSING · REVOKED. Fail closed on expired authority, incompatible versions, missing evidence, revoked consent, broken dependencies, or context outside the component’s declared scope.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. REUSE THE CONTROL—NOT THE ASSUMPTION. REGULATED COMPONENTS ARE REUSABLE ONLY WHEN CONTEXT, EVIDENCE, AUTHORITY, VERSION, AND INVALIDATION TRAVEL WITH THEM.

FINAL PROPOSITION
REGULATED COMPONENTS ARE REUSABLE ONLY WHEN CONTEXT, EVIDENCE, AUTHORITY, VERSION, AND INVALIDATION TRAVEL WITH THEM.
