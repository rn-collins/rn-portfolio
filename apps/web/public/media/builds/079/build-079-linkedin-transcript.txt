BUILD 079. A SEARCH RESULT IS NOT AN EVIDENCE MAP.. A keyword match can surface a fictional record and still miss the question it appears to answer.

SCENE 2
ASK THE QUESTION IN DIMENSIONS. QUESTION · POPULATION · CONTEXT · OUTCOME. The synthetic question opens into the exact dimensions required to test relevance without inventing certainty.

SCENE 3
QUALIFY THE EVIDENCE. TYPE · QUALITY · APPLICABILITY. Evidence type, source quality, and categorical applicability remain distinct; no score or ranking is produced.

SCENE 4
RETAIN WHAT DOES NOT FIT. SOURCE · LIMIT · UNKNOWN. Out-of-scope records, unmatched fields, limitations, and unknown applicability stay visible.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. NAVIGATION DOES NOT DECIDE. Synthetic evidence only; no patient, treatment, diagnosis, dosage, safety, effectiveness, advice, or clinical-applicability claim.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. FIND WHAT ANSWERS THE QUESTION.. AN EVIDENCE MAP ORGANIZES THE QUESTION, QUALIFIES THE RECORD, AND KEEPS EVERY SOURCE, LIMIT, AND UNKNOWN ATTACHED.
