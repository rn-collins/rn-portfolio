BUILD 033. TEST THE SYSTEM WITHOUT TEACHING THE ATTACK. Defensive exploration should expose weak assumptions, controls, and recovery paths while withholding reusable harmful instructions.

SCENE 2
BOUND THE EXERCISE. ASSET · THREAT · SCOPE · AUTHORITY. Name what is protected, the permitted environment, synthetic inputs, accountable owner, stop conditions, and prohibited actions.

SCENE 3
MODEL FAILURE. PRESSURE THE CONTROL—NOT REAL PEOPLE. Use abstract scenarios for misuse, manipulation, leakage, privilege, evasion, dependency failure, and unsafe automation.

SCENE 4
CONTAIN THE TEST. ISOLATE · MINIMIZE · MONITOR · STOP. Keep production data, credentials, live targets, personal information, and uncontrolled external effects outside the exercise.

SCENE 5
TURN FINDINGS INTO DEFENSE. DETECT · REPAIR · RETEST · RECORD. Assign severity, evidence, owner, deadline, mitigation, rollback, verification, disclosure route, and residual risk.

SCENE 6
