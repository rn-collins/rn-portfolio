SCENE 1
BUILD 025. A USE CASE IS NOT AN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN. A promising capability can still outrun the organization expected to operate it.

SCENE 2
CONSEQUENCE. START WITH WHO CAN BE AFFECTED. Severity, rights, reversibility, scale, and uncertainty determine how much readiness is required.

SCENE 3
WORKFLOW. TRACE THE REAL WORK. Map intake, data, model, review, decision, action, monitoring, and every accountable handoff.

SCENE 4
EVIDENCE. TEST THE VENDOR CLAIMS. A procurement adjective is not proof. Keep claim, scope, evidence, control, contract, and recourse inspectable.

SCENE 5
OPERATING CAPACITY. OWNER · AUTHORITY · SKILL · TIME · EXIT. A control exists only when named people can run it, stop the use, correct failure, and sustain the work.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. DESIRE TO DEPLOY IS NOT READINESS. READINESS IS THE ABILITY TO OPERATE THE CONTROLS—NOT THE DESIRE TO DEPLOY.

FINAL PROPOSITION
READINESS IS THE ABILITY TO OPERATE THE CONTROLS—NOT THE DESIRE TO DEPLOY.
