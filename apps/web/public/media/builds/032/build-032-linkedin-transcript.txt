SCENE 1
BUILD 032. A PASSING SCORE DOES NOT AUTHORIZE RELEASE. Consequential AI needs inspectable evidence that the use, workflow, people, vendor, data, and operating controls can hold under pressure.

SCENE 2
BOUND THE RELEASE. NAME THE VERSION AND THE CONSEQUENCE. Lock the model, data, configuration, purpose, affected people, decision, environment, change set, and unacceptable outcomes.

SCENE 3
RUN THE GATES. EVIDENCE · CONTROL · OWNER · AUTHORITY. Test performance, workflow, privacy, security, accessibility, notice, review, appeal, incident response, monitoring, and rollback.

SCENE 4
TEST FAILURE. EXPECTED · EDGE · MISUSE · OUTAGE. Use predeclared scenarios, thresholds, evidence locators, unresolved gaps, and independent challenge—not a launch-day demonstration.

SCENE 5
DECIDE THE NEXT STATE. HOLD · REMEDIATE · PILOT · RELEASE · ROLLBACK. Record who can decide, what conditions apply, when the decision expires, what will be monitored, and what automatically stops the use.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. READINESS MUST SURVIVE CONTACT WITH OPERATIONS. A RELEASE IS READY ONLY WHEN THE CONTROLS ARE OPERABLE, EVIDENCED, AND OWNED.
