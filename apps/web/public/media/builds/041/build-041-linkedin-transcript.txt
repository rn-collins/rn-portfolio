SCENE 1
BUILD 041. AI WORK CAN EXIST WITHOUT AN OWNER. A tool can enter a workflow, influence a decision, or depend on a vendor while responsibility remains assumed, fragmented, or invisible.

SCENE 2
MAP THE ORGANIZATION. TOOL · DECISION · VENDOR · WORKFLOW. Use synthetic records to connect each AI component to its purpose, accountable role, approving authority, operator, affected workflow, and review date.

SCENE 3
FIND THE ORPHANS. NO OWNER · STALE OWNER · SPLIT AUTHORITY. Flag missing assignments, departed owners, conflicting roles, expired reviews, unowned vendor changes, and decisions nobody is authorized to make.

SCENE 4
REASSIGN EXPLICITLY. FROM · TO · AUTHORITY · EFFECTIVE DATE. A reassignment must preserve the prior owner, reason, approval, scope, evidence, effective time, open duties, and a path to challenge or correct it.

SCENE 5
KEEP THE CLAIM BOUNDED. A MAP IS NOT ACCOUNTABILITY. Visible assignments do not prove oversight, competence, compliance, safety, performance, or that the named person accepted and exercised the role.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. MAKE OWNERSHIP VISIBLE. ACCOUNTABILITY BEGINS WHEN EVERY AI TOOL, DECISION, VENDOR, AND WORKFLOW HAS A VISIBLE OWNER.

FINAL PROPOSITION
ACCOUNTABILITY BEGINS WHEN EVERY AI TOOL, DECISION, VENDOR, AND WORKFLOW HAS A VISIBLE OWNER.
