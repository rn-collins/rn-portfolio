BUILD 062-B — The Interview Is More Than a Transcript

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 062. A TRANSCRIPT IS NOT THE WHOLE INTERVIEW. Text alone can hide who labeled a passage, which claim was asserted, what evidence was linked, where accounts conflict, and what still needs follow-up.

SCENE 2
USE A SYNTHETIC RECORD. PASSAGE · ASSERTION · SOURCE · LABEL. No real person, audio, or voice is present. Preserve fictional speaker labels, transcript segments, timestamps, recorder, version, provenance, uncertainty, and missing material.

SCENE 3
BUILD THE NETWORK. PEOPLE · CLAIMS · THEMES · EVIDENCE. Connect each recorded assertion to its exact passage and declared source. Keep analyst themes, source evidence, contradictions, and unresolved follow-up questions as different object types.

SCENE 4
KEEP CONFLICT VISIBLE. SUPPORTS · CONTRADICTS · UNKNOWN · ASK. Do not resolve disagreement by averaging or choosing a speaker. Preserve competing accounts, provenance, scope, uncertainty, and the next question required.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. A NETWORK IS NOT VERIFICATION. The demo cannot identify a person or speaker, verify transcription, intent, truth, causation, consent, rights, credibility, endorsement, admissibility, representativeness, or theme completeness.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. KEEP THE SOURCE ATTACHED. AN INTERVIEW BECOMES REVIEWABLE WHEN PASSAGES, ASSERTIONS, THEMES, EVIDENCE, CONTRADICTIONS, PROVENANCE, AND FOLLOW-UPS REMAIN DISTINCT.

FINAL PROPOSITION
AN INTERVIEW BECOMES REVIEWABLE WHEN PASSAGES, ASSERTIONS, THEMES, EVIDENCE, CONTRADICTIONS, PROVENANCE, AND FOLLOW-UPS REMAIN DISTINCT.
