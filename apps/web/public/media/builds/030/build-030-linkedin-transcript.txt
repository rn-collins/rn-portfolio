SCENE 1
BUILD 030. PASSING A CHECKLIST IS NOT AN INCLUSIVE EXPERIENCE. A screen can satisfy isolated rules while real people still cannot perceive, understand, navigate, recover, or finish.

SCENE 2
SIMULATE THE TASK. TEST THE WHOLE JOURNEY. Follow entry, orientation, action, feedback, error, recovery, completion, and the handoff beyond the interface.

SCENE 3
CHANGE THE CONDITIONS. VISION · HEARING · MOTOR · COGNITIVE · SPEECH. Add device, bandwidth, language, stress, interruption, assistive technology, and combinations of access needs.

SCENE 4
FIND THE BARRIER. NAME WHERE THE TASK BREAKS. Record the affected person, blocked action, evidence, consequence, workaround, owner, and severity without impersonating lived experience.

SCENE 5
REPAIR AND RETEST. FIX THE SYSTEM—NOT THE PERSON. Change structure, language, control, feedback, timing, alternatives, and support; then rerun the same task and preserve the result.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. ACCESS MEANS COMPLETING THE WORK. ACCESSIBILITY IS NOT A VIEW OF THE INTERFACE. IT IS WHETHER PEOPLE CAN COMPLETE THE WORK.
