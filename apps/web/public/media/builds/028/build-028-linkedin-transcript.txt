SCENE 1
BUILD 028. STORED IS NOT FOUND. A fact can exist in the archive and still fail the person who needs it at the moment of decision.

SCENE 2
INTENT. START WITH THE QUESTION. Model the language people actually use, the task they are doing, and the consequence of retrieving the wrong thing.

SCENE 3
STRUCTURE. GIVE KNOWLEDGE MORE THAN A TITLE. Stable identity, relationships, provenance, dates, audiences, and status make retrieval inspectable.

SCENE 4
RETRIEVAL. RANK RELEVANCE WITH AUTHORITY. Similarity alone is not enough. Preserve source quality, freshness, scope, contradiction, and uncertainty.

SCENE 5
EVALUATION. TEST WHAT PEOPLE CAN ACTUALLY FIND. Use known questions, missed results, false confidence, correction paths, and human feedback to measure the system.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. FINDING IS PART OF KNOWING. KNOWLEDGE YOU CANNOT RELIABLY FIND IS KNOWLEDGE YOU CANNOT RELIABLY USE.

FINAL PROPOSITION
KNOWLEDGE YOU CANNOT RELIABLY FIND IS KNOWLEDGE YOU CANNOT RELIABLY USE.
