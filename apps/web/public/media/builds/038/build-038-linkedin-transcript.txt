SCENE 1
BUILD 038. A BENCHMARK SCORE IS NOT GENERAL PERFORMANCE. A model can look strong on synthetic examples and still fail the domain, workflow, people, conditions, or consequence that matter.

SCENE 2
BOUND THE CLAIM. NAME THE USE AND THE DECISION. Lock model and version, domain task, population, environment, human role, success criteria, unacceptable outcomes, and what the test cannot prove.

SCENE 3
BUILD THE SET. REPRESENTATIVE · EDGE · FAILURE · ABSTAIN. Use synthetic cases with provenance, coverage rationale, protected challenge sets, known limitations, and competent domain review.

SCENE 4
MEASURE SEPARATELY. QUALITY · SAFETY · FAIRNESS · RELIABILITY. Keep uncertainty, subgroup behavior, calibration, refusals, consistency, latency, cost, and human-workflow effects visible instead of one average.

SCENE 5
INTERPRET WITH CONTROL. EVIDENCE · LIMIT · OWNER · NEXT TEST. Preserve errors and contradictions, investigate causes, compare alternatives, define monitoring and stops, and never let the score approve deployment.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. THE CLAIM CANNOT OUTRUN THE TEST. A DOMAIN EVALUATION SUPPORTS ONLY THE USE, DATA, CONDITIONS, AND CONSEQUENCES IT ACTUALLY TESTED.

FINAL PROPOSITION
