BUILD 004 — Before the Automation

SCENE 1
BUILD 004. SOURCES ARE NOT INTELLIGENCE. Explicit gates create the transformation.

SCENE 2
COLLECT. REGISTER THE SOURCE. Issuer, version, dates, exact locator.

SCENE 3
SEPARATE. SOURCE ≠ ANALYST CLAIM. Interpretation needs its own object.

SCENE 4
VERIFY. UNVERIFIED STAYS OUT. Keep contradiction and uncertainty visible.

SCENE 5
BRIEF. SYNTHESIS FOLLOWS STATUS. Never promote unchecked claims.

SCENE 6
AUTOMATION. SEE THE METHOD FIRST. Then automate the inspectable parts.

FINAL PROPOSITION
Automate the method only after you can see the method.
