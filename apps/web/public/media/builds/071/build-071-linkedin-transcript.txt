BUILD 071-B — “Legal” Does Not Mean Accessible

Silent-first LinkedIn cut transcript

SCENE 1
BUILD 071. LEGAL DOES NOT MEAN ACCESSIBLE. A fictional legal-status signal changes. That does not establish practical medical-cannabis access for any person.

SCENE 2
OPEN THE ACCESS CHAIN. EVERY GATE CAN CHANGE THE PRACTICAL ANSWER. Separate eligibility, clinician availability, geography and travel, supply, cost and payment, access needs, consent, privacy, and evidence dates.

SCENE 3
KEEP SIGNALS DISTINCT. OPEN · UNRESOLVED · CONSTRAINED · UNKNOWN. A policy record cannot locate a clinician, shorten travel, create supply, cover cost, remove barriers, or prove eligibility.

SCENE 4
PRESERVE ACCESS NEEDS. DISABILITY · LANGUAGE · DIGITAL. Accommodation and privacy reviews remain explicit instead of being hidden behind a single market or program status.

SCENE 5
BOUND THE RESULT. A DASHBOARD IS NOT A CARE OR LEGAL DETERMINATION. No real patient data, diagnosis, treatment, dosage, product recommendation, clinician verification, safety, effectiveness, eligibility, or access claim appears.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. TRACK THE DISTANCE BETWEEN LEGAL AND REACHABLE. ACCESS INTELLIGENCE BEGINS WHEN STATUS, ELIGIBILITY, CARE, PLACE, SUPPLY, COST, ACCESS NEEDS, EVIDENCE, BARRIERS, AND UNKNOWN STATE STAY CONNECTED.

FINAL PROPOSITION
TRACK THE DISTANCE BETWEEN LEGAL AND REACHABLE.
