BUILD 051-B — Adaptive Cannabis Source Navigator

1. ONE QUESTION DOES NOT NEED EVERY LESSON. A fictional learner’s topic can change source order without creating personal advice.
2. READ THE QUESTION—NOT THE PERSON. Collect no health profile; preserve topic, source date, jurisdiction and unknowns.
3. REORDER SOURCES, NOT ANSWERS. Adapt navigation and knowledge checks; do not recommend use, products or doses.
4. STOP AT INDIVIDUAL RISK. High-risk or legal questions route to current official or professional help.
5. EDUCATION IS NOT CLEARANCE. A dated source cannot determine personal safety, effectiveness, impairment or legality.
6. KEEP THE SOURCE AND LIMIT TOGETHER. Every lesson should expose issuer, date, exact section and unresolved uncertainty.

LIMITATION
Dated source-navigation demonstration only. Current official sources must be rechecked before reliance.
