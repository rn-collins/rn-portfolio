SCENE 1
BUILD 031. A PRIVACY POLICY IS NOT PRODUCT BEHAVIOR. The real privacy posture appears in defaults, data flows, permissions, retention, inference, sharing, deletion, and failure states.

SCENE 2
OBSERVE THE JOURNEY. TRACE DATA FROM ENTRY TO EXIT. Map collection, purpose, transformation, storage, access, disclosure, reuse, model input, retention, and verified deletion.

SCENE 3
TEST THE DEFAULTS. WHAT HAPPENS BEFORE A PERSON CHOOSES?. Optional collection, tracking, sharing, personalization, and sensitive inference should not quietly activate through design or fatigue.

SCENE 4
COMPARE THE PROMISE. CLAIM · CONTROL · EVIDENCE · RESULT. Test whether notice matches runtime behavior, the control changes processing, and the outcome remains visible and correctable.

SCENE 5
DESIGN RECOURSE. ACCESS · CORRECT · WITHDRAW · DELETE · APPEAL. Every route needs an owner, deadline, identity safeguards, downstream handling, exception basis, status, and escalation.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. READ THE PRODUCT THROUGH ITS BEHAVIOR. PRIVACY IS WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES WHEN NOBODY IS READING THE POLICY.

FINAL PROPOSITION
PRIVACY IS WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES WHEN NOBODY IS READING THE POLICY.
