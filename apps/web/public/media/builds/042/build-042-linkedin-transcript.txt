SCENE 1
BUILD 042. A PROMPT IS NOT A JOB DESCRIPTION. An agent can produce fluent work while its purpose, authority, tools, accountable owner, prohibited actions, and required review remain undefined.

SCENE 2
DEFINE THE ROLE. PURPOSE · INPUT · OUTPUT · OWNER. Use a synthetic role to name the bounded task, accepted inputs, expected artifact, responsible human, affected people, success criteria, and consequence.

SCENE 3
DECLARE THE TOOL ENVELOPE. ALLOW · DENY · REQUIRE APPROVAL. Assign only necessary tools and data. Separate read, draft, recommend, communicate, transact, and delete permissions; attach evidence and expiry.

SCENE 4
MOVE A RESPONSIBILITY. PERMISSION · RISK · EVALUATION. When work moves in or out, recompute required access, consequence, evaluation coverage, human review, inherited ownership, and the safe fallback.

SCENE 5
GOVERN THE EDGE. REVIEW · ESCALATE · ABSTAIN · STOP. Require review before consequential action. Preserve uncertainty, failed tools, boundary violations, escalation routes, termination rules, and exact state-only replay.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. GIVE THE AGENT A REAL JOB. AN AI AGENT HAS A REAL JOB ONLY WHEN ITS ROLE, TOOLS, BOUNDARIES, REVIEW POINTS, AND STOP CONDITIONS ARE EXPLICIT.
