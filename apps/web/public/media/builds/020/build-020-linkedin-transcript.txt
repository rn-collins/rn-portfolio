SCENE 1
BUILD 020. A DECISION IS NOT A SNAPSHOT.. Accountability requires the actor, authority, time, evidence, alternatives, acknowledgement, result, and corrections.

SCENE 2
RECONSTRUCT. LINK THE COMPLETE RECORD.. Open one decision into a temporal chain instead of leaving its rationale scattered.

SCENE 3
EVIDENCE. REMOVAL MUST EXPOSE THE GAP.. A ledger should show when supporting evidence is missing—not silently preserve a false sense of completeness.

SCENE 4
CORRECT. APPEND. SUPERSEDE. NEVER ERASE.. Corrections preserve the earlier record, the change, its author, its time, and its reason.

SCENE 5
BOUNDARY. PROVENANCE ≠ PROOF.. A complete sequence does not prove truth, causation, legal authority, compliance, or a correct decision.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. KEEP THE WHO · WHAT · WHEN · WHY. An accountable decision remains reconstructable after the moment has passed.

FINAL PROPOSITION
AN ACCOUNTABLE DECISION REMAINS RECONSTRUCTABLE AFTER THE MOMENT HAS PASSED.
