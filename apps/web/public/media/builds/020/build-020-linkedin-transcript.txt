BUILD 020 — Open the Decision

SCENE 1
BUILD 020. A DECISION IS NOT A SNAPSHOT. Accountability needs a sequence.

SCENE 2
RECONSTRUCT. LINK THE COMPLETE RECORD. Actor, authority, time, evidence, alternatives, result.

SCENE 3
EVIDENCE. REMOVAL MUST EXPOSE GAPS. Never preserve false completeness.

SCENE 4
CORRECT. APPEND. SUPERSEDE. NEVER ERASE. Keep author, time, reason, prior record.

SCENE 5
BOUNDARY. PROVENANCE ≠ PROOF. Sequence cannot prove truth or correctness.

SCENE 6
SAVE. KEEP WHO. WHAT. WHEN. WHY. Make decisions reconstructable.

FINAL PROPOSITION
An accountable decision remains reconstructable after the moment passes.
