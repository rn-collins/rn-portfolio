SCENE 3
DEFINE THE FIELDS. TYPE · REQUIRED · CONSTRAINT. Every field earns a meaning and a rule.

SCENE 4
CONNECT THE GRAPH. FROM · RELATIONSHIP · TO. Cardinality makes vague connections testable.

SCENE 5
GROUND THE FACTS. AUTHORITY · LOCATOR · FRESHNESS. Source rules belong in the skeleton—not as cleanup later.

SCENE 6
SAVE THIS. STRUCTURE IS NOT TRUTH.. It makes assumptions visible enough to validate, challenge, and improve.

FINAL PROPOSITION
IF THE IDEA HAS NO STRUCTURE, IT CANNOT BE TESTED.
