Who we are

This site is operated by Rayven-Nikkita Collins LLC, doing business as RN Collins, based in Honolulu, Hawaii. References to "we," "us," or "this practice" in this policy refer to Rayven-Nikkita Collins LLC.

What information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Submit a contact or inquiry form (name, email address, message, inquiry type)
  • Purchase a product through a linked third-party platform (handled by that platform)
  • Book a call through our scheduling link

We do not collect payment information directly. Payment is processed by third-party platforms (Gumroad, Stripe). Their privacy policies govern any payment data.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and product questions
  • To deliver products or resources you have purchased or requested
  • For internal record-keeping and engagement tracking

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How information is stored

Form submissions and contact inquiries are stored in Upstash Redis (a cloud key-value database) and delivered via Slack notifications to our internal workspace. Scheduling information is managed through Microsoft Bookings (Northeastern University).

All data is handled within the United States.

Third-party tools

This site uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Vercel (site hosting)
  • Upstash (form submission storage)
  • Resend (transactional email delivery)
  • Gumroad (product delivery and payment)
  • Microsoft Bookings (scheduling)
  • Anthropic API (AI functionality in deployed tools)

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated.
