## Bottom line

**Yes—but not as 100 equally mature products.** The collection contains a distinctive RN method: abstract claims are repeatedly converted into inspectable systems of actors, evidence, permissions, handoffs, consequences, uncertainty, and stopping conditions. The strongest original through-lines are implementation versus authorization, lived access versus policy status, responsibility between institutions, place-sensitive transfer, provenance, ownership, and human authority.

The present evidence does **not** support describing all 100 as frontier AI systems, validated research, or equally substantive standalone products. The most serious gap is source and field evidence. Only 6 builds contain direct real external authority links in the build-specific material inspected; 2 use explicit synthetic placeholders; 92 do not demonstrate direct external source lineage in those files.

## What is actually distinctive

1. **Decision architecture over slogans.** “Human in the loop,” “access,” “compliance,” “personalization,” and “resilience” are decomposed into named actors, permissions, evidence, triggers, consequences, and failure paths.
2. **Implementation is treated as a separate object of inquiry.** Laws, policies, tools, and services are repeatedly tested against staffing, money, geography, infrastructure, handoffs, and lived use.
3. **Power and ownership are product requirements.** The work asks who owns the asset, who can act, who bears risk, who created value, and who can contest a decision.
4. **Place and embodiment matter.** Hawaiʻi/island conditions, sensory experience, institutional burden, disability/access, and nervous-system conditions appear as structural variables rather than decoration.
5. **Boundary honesty is unusually consistent.** Later builds repeatedly distinguish synthetic rehearsal from real-world truth, prediction, care, legal advice, authority, or safety.

## What weakens the claim

- Many builds are variations on a shared synthetic-fixture and control-panel scaffold.
- Standard infrastructure topics—authentication, RBAC, APIs, testing, deployment, telemetry, portability, agent roles—are useful but not independently original.
- Conceptual lineage is declared across all 100, but declared dependency is not proof that one build actually executes or integrates another.
- Synthetic fixtures test deterministic logic; they do not establish that the problem exists at scale, that users need the solution, or that the intervention improves outcomes.
- Source coverage is too thin for the breadth of legal, health, regulatory, accessibility, cannabis, psychedelic, and public-systems claims.

## Decision

Continue the project only as a **curated research-and-design laboratory**. Stop equal-weight treatment and Canva scaling. The next defensible tranche is to take the four “hero now” builds and a small selection of “hero after evidence” builds through exact-source research, user/field interviews, disconfirming evidence, observed testing, and complete reproduction records. The remaining builds should function as supporting modules, grouped systems, or archive material.
