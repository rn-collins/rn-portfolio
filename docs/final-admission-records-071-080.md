## 071 — Can I Actually Get Cannabis Care? — REFRAME Medical Cannabis Access Intelligence Dashboard
**Public question:** Legal where I live—but can I realistically access care?
**Novice path:** choose a location and practical constraints; see the real path, likely barriers, missing links, and what to verify next.
**Expert depth:** eligibility, clinician access, geography, supply, product availability, cost, transportation, disability access, policy, market structure, data freshness.
**A technological ceiling:** jurisdiction-aware rules graph; geospatial provider/access layers; travel-time analysis; supply/availability feeds where lawful/reliable; cost ranges; temporal freshness; accessibility constraints; scenario engine; provenance; uncertainty; privacy-preserving user context. No individualized medical advice.
**B-Web:** interactive access map/path where toggling transportation, island/location, cost, mobility, clinician availability or supply visibly changes reachable care.
**B-LinkedIn:** “LEGAL” appears, then barrier after barrier inserts itself between the word and the patient.
**Saveable payload:** `LEGAL ≠ AVAILABLE ≠ AFFORDABLE ≠ REACHABLE ≠ USABLE`.
