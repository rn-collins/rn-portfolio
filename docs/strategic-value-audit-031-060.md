## Replacement-candidate directions emerging from this batch

### R-F — AI Vendor Evidence Room
A structured workspace that converts marketing claims into evidence requests, validation status, unresolved risks, contract/procurement questions, and deployment conditions.

### R-G — Neurotechnology / Human-State Claims Auditor
A tool for assessing claims that a product improves focus, calm, cognition, stress, creativity, or wellbeing: mechanism, evidence, population, uncertainty, measurement and commercial overreach.

### R-H — Physical Space → Human State Audit
An evidence-aware assessment for workplaces, schools, third spaces, clinics, homes or hospitality that maps sensory/environmental conditions to likely human experience and testable design changes.
