## Storyboard and on-screen transcript

The film is silent-first. All meaning is present as on-screen text; these six scenes run for three seconds each.

1. **A STRAIN NAME IS NOT A PRODUCT MODEL.** The same or similar label can conceal different formats, declared composition, batches, evidence, contexts, and unknowns.
2. **COMPOSITION · FORMAT · CONTEXT.** Use exact-bound synthetic products and relative bands. Keep label, goal, constraints, missing evidence, and NOT COLLECTED person context visible.
3. **LET THE DIMENSIONS REORGANIZE.** When the learning goal or comparison pair changes, reorganize only the visible dimensions. Do not turn similarity into equivalence, effect, quality, or fit.
4. **DECLARED · SYNTHETIC · UNKNOWN.** A displayed band is not a dose, potency result, laboratory analysis, verified label, batch record, predicted experience, or current legal status.
5. **COMPARE—DO NOT RECOMMEND.** The demo cannot rank products, match one to a person, predict effects, guide purchase or use, interpret a certificate, or establish safety, legality, or effectiveness.
6. **LOOK BEYOND THE NAME.** A NAME CANNOT CARRY COMPOSITION, FORMAT, CONTEXT, UNCERTAINTY, OR A PERSON'S GOALS.
