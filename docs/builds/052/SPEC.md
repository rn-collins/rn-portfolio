## Product and contract

Version A compares two of three exact-bound synthetic products across label, format, and declared relative composition bands while keeping missing evidence and person context visible. Version B shows how the visible dimensions reorganize similarity. Engine `052.2.0` accepts only one exact seven-key comparison fixture, two distinct closed-set product IDs, one closed learning goal, `NOT_COLLECTED` person context, the exact ordered product fixture registry, and the exact inherited `cap:051` / engine `051.2.0` dated source-and-safety contract.

Missing, duplicate, reordered, extended, or altered product records fail closed. The inherited contract is run through Build 051 validation, so malformed, stale, future-checked, or transitioned source state also fails closed. The 2026-11-12 federal-hemp future-status boundary remains inherited. Export is atomic and state-only and contains both complete registries.

The engine never ranks, recommends, predicts, matches a product to a person, or interprets real product data. Its declared synthetic bands are not verified composition, dose, potency, laboratory results, effects, or evidence of safety, quality, effectiveness, legality, or equivalence.

## Boundaries

Not medical or legal advice, dosing or use guidance, product verification, laboratory interpretation, purchase guidance, or a recommendation. No real product, batch, certificate, person, health condition, experience, or jurisdiction is represented. Real use requires freshly checked current primary sources and cannabis-science, public-health, analytical-testing, labeling, consumer-protection, legal, lived-experience, privacy, safety, and accessibility review.
