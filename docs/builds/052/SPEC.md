# Build 052 — Beyond the Strain Name

## Canon

- Job: Compare cannabis products and context using composition, format, experience, goals and constraints instead of labels alone.
- B-Web: The strain label collapses as richer dimensions reorganize product similarity.
- Uses: 028, 049, 051.
- Creates: `cap:052`.
- Direct consumers: 096, 097.

## Product and contract

Version A compares two of three exact-bound synthetic products across label, format, and declared relative composition bands while keeping missing evidence and person context visible. Version B shows how the visible dimensions reorganize similarity. Engine `052.1.0` accepts only one exact five-key comparison fixture, two distinct closed-set product IDs, one closed learning goal, and `NOT_COLLECTED` person context.

The engine never ranks, recommends, predicts, matches a product to a person, or interprets real product data. Its synthetic bands are not dose, potency, laboratory results, effects, or evidence of safety, quality, effectiveness, legality, or equivalence. Export is atomic and state-only.

## Boundaries

Not medical or legal advice, dosing or use guidance, product verification, laboratory interpretation, purchase guidance, or a recommendation. No real product, batch, certificate, person, health condition, experience, or jurisdiction is represented. Real use requires current primary sources and cannabis-science, public-health, analytical-testing, labeling, consumer-protection, legal, lived-experience, privacy, safety, and accessibility review.
