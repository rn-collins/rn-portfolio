## Integrity model — engine 032.2.0

This is a deterministic schema and blocker demonstration over synthetic evidence. `READINESS RECORD COMPLETE` means only that every registered record satisfies the fixture’s structural rules. It is not approval, deployment authorization, safety certification, legal compliance, or validation that the evidence is true, representative, reproducible, applicable, or sufficient.

The release record pins the complete system/model version, consequential-use scope, assessment date, and change trace. Eight required gates are unique, cannot be downgraded, and explicitly depend on Builds 012, 013, 020, 030, and 031 plus local fallback, stop/escalation, and incident-owner records. Unregistered gates cannot substitute.

Each gate has critical/high severity, explicit residual risk, accountable evidence owner, and one evidence object with unique identity, dependency source, scope, method, pass/fail/inconclusive outcome, checked date, expiry, and optional revocation. Future-checked, expired, revoked, missing, duplicated, inconclusive, mismatched-source, high-risk, and unknown-risk records block. A label or checkbox is not evidence.

Rollback requires feasibility plus an identified, completed, timed test. Human review requires a named person, bounded authority scope, valid dates, and no revocation; it remains distinct from formal release authorization. Changes in model, system, workflow, population, operating environment, or risk require applicability review and usually new evidence.

The local deterministic export contains original state, derived result, engine version, gate order, exact replay input, canonical metadata, and non-claims. Do not enter production, personal, confidential, security-sensitive, or privileged evidence.

032-B mirrors the record sequence with reversible native buttons, text status, a non-hidden diagram description, visible focus, responsive layout, and reduced-motion handling. Independent authorization, production evidence reproduction, red-team/security/legal review, affected-person and operator testing, physical-device/manual accessibility, authenticated browser, GitHub-hosted CI, and LinkedIn media verification remain external gates.
