# Build 100 — Island Resilience Scenario & Decision Room

## Job
Model an island shock, cascading consequences, intervention options, tradeoffs and accountable decisions.

A is a bounded deterministic scenario rehearsal. B converges the full series into a decision system and lets the visitor zoom backward through its direct lineage.

## Safety boundary
Frozen synthetic scenarios only. This is not a forecast, emergency instruction, resource-allocation authority or operational system.
