## Boundaries

Every person/speaker label, passage, transcript segment, timestamp, assertion, theme, evidence link, contradiction, provenance link, consent/rights record, and follow-up is synthetic. Labels are identifiers inside the fixture, not identity or speaker identification. Transcript text is recorded fixture content, not evidence of transcription accuracy, speech, intent, truth, causation, credibility, endorsement, representativeness, or what anyone actually said. Analyst themes remain labels, not exhaustive or authoritative findings. The interface cannot establish identity, transcription accuracy, intent, truth, causation, consent validity, rights, license, permission, confidentiality, theme completeness, credibility, endorsement, admissibility, representativeness, biometric identity, voice match, diarization accuracy, speaker identification, or authority. It performs no biometric processing or AI voice detection. Real use requires valid consent/rights, secure source preservation, correction/withdrawal paths, affected-person review, and competent qualitative-research, editorial, legal, privacy/biometric, records/provenance, ethics, security, lived-experience, and accessibility review.
