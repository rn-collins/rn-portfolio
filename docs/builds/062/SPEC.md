# Build 062 — The Interview Is More Than a Transcript

## Canon
- Job: Turn interviews into people, claims, themes, evidence, contradictions and follow-up questions.
- B-Web: A transcript becomes a source-linked knowledge network with audio and text provenance.
- Uses: 011, 028, 058.
- Creates: `cap:062`.
- Consumers: 063, 080, 082.

## Contract
Engine `062.1.0` uses only fixed synthetic audio/text segments. Speaker labels and identity assertions, segment timing, audio/text source and version, transcript text, edits, claims, themes, evidence links, recorded tensions, uncertainty, consent/rights state and follow-ups remain distinct. Timing conflict, unknown rights and missing evidence fail closed to review/hold/gap states.

Exact inherited contracts: `011.1.0`; `028.2.0` / `transparent-ranking-v2` / `derived-concept-map-v2`; and `058.1.0`, preserving identity, relevance/classification and support-linkage limits. Recursive exact validation is serialization-free and no-throw for malformed, duplicate, reordered, extra, non-finite, BigInt, proxy/accessor and serialization-hook inputs.

## Boundaries
No real audio, text, interview, person, identity, consent, right or evidence is represented. No biometric or speaker identification/inference. Not proof of identity, verbatim accuracy, intent, truth, causation, consent validity, rights or permission, theme completeness, contradiction, credibility, endorsement, admissibility or representativeness. Real use requires informed consent and purpose controls, secure source/audio retention, transcript and edit verification, participant correction/withdrawal paths, evidence review, privacy/security/accessibility controls and competent qualitative-method review.
