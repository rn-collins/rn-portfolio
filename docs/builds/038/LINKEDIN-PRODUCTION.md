## Storyboard and on-screen transcript

The film is silent-first. All meaning is present as on-screen text; the following six scenes run for three seconds each.

1. **A BENCHMARK SCORE IS NOT GENERAL PERFORMANCE.** A model can look strong on synthetic examples and still fail the domain, workflow, people, conditions, or consequence that matter.
2. **NAME THE USE AND THE DECISION.** Lock model and version, domain task, population, environment, human role, success criteria, unacceptable outcomes, and what the test cannot prove.
3. **REPRESENTATIVE · EDGE · FAILURE · ABSTAIN.** Use synthetic cases with provenance, coverage rationale, protected challenge sets, known limitations, and competent domain review.
4. **QUALITY · SAFETY · FAIRNESS · RELIABILITY.** Keep uncertainty, subgroup behavior, calibration, refusals, consistency, latency, cost, and human-workflow effects visible instead of one average.
5. **EVIDENCE · LIMIT · OWNER · NEXT TEST.** Preserve errors and contradictions, investigate causes, compare alternatives, define monitoring and stops, and never let the score approve deployment.
6. **THE CLAIM CANNOT OUTRUN THE TEST.** A domain evaluation supports only the use, data, conditions, and consequences it actually tested.

## Rights, privacy, and accessibility

Original deterministic typography and layout by Rayven-Nikkita Collins / RN Builds. No third-party footage, images, music, voice, logos, or personal likenesses are used.
