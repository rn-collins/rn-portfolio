# Build 024 specification

## Frozen canon

- Title: Privacy & Data Minimization Engine
- Job: Determines which data is necessary, optional, sensitive, derived, prohibited, retained, or deletable.
- B-Web: A bloated intake shrinks to its justified minimum.
- Uses: 023
- Creates: cap:024
- Later consumers: 030, 031, 036, 049, 067, 083, 091, 097 and 099

## Derived implementation

The question, thesis, fixture, thresholds, labels, and interface copy are derived choices. Engine 024.2.0 requires unique field identity, a purpose identifier, accountable owner, inspectable evidence reference, and tested no-collection counterfactual before a necessary, sensitive, or derived field can pass. Sensitive collection and sensitive-source derivation must match an active purpose-specific permission record inherited from Build 023. Permission does not establish necessity and cannot rescue optional or prohibited collection.

Retention records include a duration, basis, trigger, and deletion method. These are planned controls, not proof that deletion occurred. Export and disclosure rules remain explicit for every collected field.

“CONTROL CHECK PASSED” means only that the synthetic inventory satisfied this version’s deterministic completeness rules. It is not a factual finding of necessity, proof of effective deletion, legal advice, or a compliance determination.
