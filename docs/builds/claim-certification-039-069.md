## Result

This lane reviewed 31 build overviews and the corresponding A/B public surfaces. The earlier blanket hold was overinclusive: 27 builds make a bounded design proposition through an explicitly synthetic or demonstrative interface and do not make a material external factual claim that needs independent empirical substantiation. Build 045 was independently reproduced against the official Hawai‘i program page and is qualified only for named official steps; every experiential barrier remains an illustrative fixture. Four builds remain held because their health, environmental-effect or care propositions need narrower claim-specific evidence.

| Total reviewed | 039–069 | 31 |

No source was treated as proof that a prototype is effective, safe, compliant, representative, or deployed. Contextual standards support the design vocabulary only. Implementation claims remain tied to the live artifact and its retained test/deployment record.

## Shared public-language findings

The following recurring statements are not external factual claims:

- “A is the working tool; B turns the same question into a film, motion argument and interactive web experience” is an inspectable implementation description.
- “RN conceived…structured…designed…implemented…” is an authorship statement, not independent-world evidence.
- “This independent prototype…is not a deployed client system, professional advice or proof of real-world outcomes” is an express limitation.
- Instructions beginning with “use,” “inspect,” “move,” “advance,” or “watch” describe the interface or invite exploration.
- Builds 055–069 expressly identify fixed, exact, fictional, modeled, or synthetic states. Their output must continue to be presented as a design demonstration rather than empirical validation.
