## Contract
Engine `058.1.0` evaluates one exact synthetic claim against three fixed roots with authority label, source date, version, locator, exact quotation or bounded proposition, and verification state. Removing a root collapses this graph’s recorded support; it does not prove falsity. Conflicts remain unresolved and missing roots remain visible.

Exact inherited contracts: ledger `020.2.0`; evidence search `028.2.0` with `transparent-ranking-v2` and `derived-concept-map-v2`; change trace `029.2.0`; provenance `057.1.0`. Relevance, classification, freshness, provenance and registered impact do not prove truth, authority, quality, applicability, sufficiency, causation, completeness, authorship, ownership, permission, authenticity or admissibility.

Recursive exact validation is serialization-free and no-throw for malformed, duplicate, reordered, extra, non-finite, BigInt, proxy/accessor and serialization-hook inputs. Export is atomic and state-only.

## Boundaries
All claims, quotations, propositions, authorities, dates, versions, locators and states are synthetic. Evidence linkage or support state is not truth, correctness, authority, causation, completeness, legal admissibility, scientific validity, consensus, applicability, sufficiency or endorsement. An exact quotation can be false, misleading, out of context, superseded or inapplicable. Real use requires primary-source retrieval, quotation/context verification, authority/version/date/freshness checks, contradiction and retraction search, domain-method review, and legal/evidentiary review where relevant.
