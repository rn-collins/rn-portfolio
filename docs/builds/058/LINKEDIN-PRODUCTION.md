## Boundaries

Every claim, evidence object, source, authority, quotation, locator, date, version, link, contradiction, removal, support state, and verification state is synthetic. The interface evaluates recorded graph linkage and declared coverage only; linkage, a citation, a source label, an authority label, corroboration, or verification status is not proof of truth, correctness, authority, causation, completeness, admissibility, scientific validity, consensus, endorsement, currentness, applicability, or real-world support. It does not perform research, fact-checking, legal analysis, scientific review, evidence grading, or source authentication. Real use requires exact primary records, date/version/currentness review, independent verification, and competent subject-matter, research-methods, legal/evidentiary, scientific/statistical, records/provenance, editorial, ethics, and accessibility review.
