# Build 073 LinkedIn production boundary

## Visual build

**Access for Whom?** redraws a fictional celebratory reform map through eight access dimensions. The motion should begin with one apparently open synthetic program, then reveal distinct cost, geography, eligibility, culture-review, disability, language, workforce, and legal-risk signals. The final proposition is: **ACCESS IS NOT A YES/NO MAP.**

## Production specification

- 720 × 900 px, 24 fps, 432 frames, exactly 18 seconds
- H.264 High, `yuv420p`, fast start; silent-first and no audio track
- Five legible scenes with high-contrast type and no meaning conveyed by color alone
- Poster and contact sheet must be generated from the canonical render
- Abstract geometry only; no external footage, real maps, people, patients, clinicians, facilities, cultural motifs, medicines, ceremonies, or tourism imagery

## Required on-screen boundary

`FULLY SYNTHETIC · NO REAL PEOPLE OR JURISDICTIONS · NO EQUITY OR ACCESS DETERMINATION · NOT LEGAL OR MEDICAL ADVICE`

The visual may describe only the fixed synthetic fixture. It must not imply current psychedelic law, eligibility, availability, equity, safety, effectiveness, cultural authority, community consent, endorsement, representativeness, or a recommended service or treatment. Culture appears only as an unresolved review dimension, never as simulated authority or knowledge.
