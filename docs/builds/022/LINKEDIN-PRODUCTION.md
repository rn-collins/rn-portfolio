## On-screen transcript and storyboard

1. **BUILD 022 — THE WORKFLOW LOOKS COMPLETE.** But the people carrying its consequences can still be missing from the map.
2. **IDENTIFY — WHO CAN BE AFFECTED?** Name each person, community, worker, customer, and bystander—not an abstract user.
3. **TRACE — WORKFLOW → HANDOFF → CONSEQUENCE.** Follow how an effect begins, travels, compounds, and reaches a real person.
4. **EVIDENCE THE PROTECTION — NOTICE · VOICE · REPRESENTATION · RECOURSE.** A checkbox is not protection. Record the basis, owner, channel, and retained evidence.
5. **KEEP THE GAPS VISIBLE — EXPOSURE · INHERITANCE · MONITORING.** Missing protections and downstream conditions must remain inspectable.
6. **SAVE THIS — MAP THE PERSON—NOT ONLY THE PROCESS.** A consequence map matters when affected people remain visible, heard, and able to seek remedy.
