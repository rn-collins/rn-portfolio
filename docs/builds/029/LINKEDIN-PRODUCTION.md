## On-screen transcript / storyboard

1. **Every change enters a system.** A policy edit, model update, data field, or workflow shortcut can alter outcomes far beyond the place it began.
2. **Name what will be different.** Record the actor, object, purpose, timing, assumptions, dependencies, and the prior state being replaced.
3. **Follow who and what it can reach.** Map direct effects, downstream handoffs, delayed effects, feedback loops, rights, and people carrying the burden.
4. **Expected · edge · failure · misuse.** Compare evidence, uncertainty, reversibility, scale, severity, and the conditions that would change the conclusion.
5. **Owner · monitor · stop · remedy.** A release needs accountable authority, observable signals, rollback criteria, notice, correction, and recourse.
6. **Follow the consequence past the change.** A change is not small when its consequences travel farther than its author can see.
