## Launch post
Most ideas do not need a launch first. They need a smaller test.

Build 086 turns a rough idea into content experiments with a declared hypothesis, requested action, success threshold and stop rule. Synthetic observations then update the next decision without being mistaken for demand or market fit.

Attention is not demand. A save is not a purchase. Stated intent is not behavior. The strongest available result is readiness for a bounded real-world test.
