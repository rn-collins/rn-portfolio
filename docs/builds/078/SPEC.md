# Build 078 — Cannabis Evidence Translation Layer

## Canonical contract

Build 078 uses Builds 002, 031, 051, and 058 and creates `cap:078`. Its exact artifacts are `Evidence Translation Protocol` and `Audience Caveat Rules`.

078-A is **Cannabis Evidence Translation Layer**: it translates scientific, legal, product, and patient evidence for distinct audiences while preserving uncertainty and source quality. Registry title: **Translate the Evidence Without Breaking It**. Job: **Explain cannabis evidence for different audiences without changing what the evidence supports.** 078-B is **Evidence Does Not Travel Intact by Accident**: a study passes through translation layers without losing its limits. On the web, evidence passes through audience lenses while truth anchors stay fixed.

## Frozen synthetic fixture

The engine admits exactly five fictional evidence cases in fixed order. Cedar is a synthetic controlled-study fixture for a public reader; Harbor is a synthetic observational dataset for a clinical educator; Lattice is a fictional legal text for a policy analyst; Mesa is an invented product-quality table for a product team; Orbit is a synthetic patient-reported archive for a patient advocate. There are no real participants, patients, products, lots, assays, laws, jurisdictions, agencies, or testimonies.

Every case preserves its source type, explicit source-quality description, claim, population, intervention or exposure, comparison, outcome, limitations, uncertainty, as-of date, target audience, explanation, and caveats. Translation may change audience-facing language but may not upgrade source quality, expand the claim, remove a limitation, resolve uncertainty, or create a real-world inference.

Only the five byte-for-byte frozen presets are admitted. Additional, mutated, real, authority-bearing, hostile, or unrecognized input returns `INVALID` and no translation. There is no free text, network dependency, live evidence or legal feed, randomness, inference about people or products, or fallback to an apparently similar case.

## UI and export contract

078-A uses native select label `TRANSLATION CASE` and buttons `RESTORE CEDAR CASE` and `EXPORT TRANSLATION RECORD`. The exact export filename is `synthetic-cannabis-evidence-translation.json`. The payload contains version `078.1.0`, exact lineage, exact artifacts, full admission block, input, result, and replay data.

078-B starts closed and uses `PASS THROUGH AUDIENCE LENS`; its live status says `Audience changed; evidence limits stayed attached`. The transformation must retain every truth anchor and caveat instead of turning accessible language into stronger evidence.

Both routes require semantic native controls, visible keyboard focus, live status, reduced-motion support, print support, and no horizontal document overflow at 320 CSS pixels. Color is never the only carrier of source quality or uncertainty.

## Evidence, legal, clinical, product, and patient boundaries

`SYNTHETIC-SOURCE-CHECKED` means only that a fixed invented record passed fixture validation. It is not verification of real evidence, law, a product, or a patient account. The layer is not current law, legal research, legal advice, clinical guidance, or a compliance determination. It provides no diagnosis, treatment, dosage, product recommendation, safety conclusion, effectiveness conclusion, risk-benefit determination, rights determination, or outcome prediction.

The demonstration cannot be used for patient care, self-treatment, purchasing, product development, labeling, marketing, legal, compliance, policy, enforcement, investment, or resource-allocation decisions. Qualified professionals must inspect current primary sources and applicable facts for real questions.

## LinkedIn boundary contract

The silent-first 18-second asset may communicate only that an invented evidence record can be explained differently while its limits remain attached. It must visibly say `SYNTHETIC EVIDENCE — NO REAL PATIENTS OR PRODUCTS` and `NO MEDICAL OR LEGAL ADVICE, DIAGNOSIS, TREATMENT, DOSAGE, SAFETY, EFFECTIVENESS, CURRENT-LAW, OR COMPLIANCE CLAIM`, with the same boundary in caption and alt text.

No real cannabis products, packages, labels, patients, clinicians, facilities, regulators, agencies, laws, citations, studies, statistics, or testimonials. No medical imagery, consumption imagery, compliance colors, authoritative checkmarks, risk grades, treatment language, recommendations, or guaranteed outcomes.
