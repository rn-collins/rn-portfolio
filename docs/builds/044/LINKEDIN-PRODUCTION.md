## Storyboard and on-screen transcript

The film is silent-first. All meaning is present as on-screen text; these six scenes run for three seconds each.

1. **RECURRING REQUIREMENTS ARE NOT COPY-PASTE BLOCKS.** Approvals, consent, evidence, disclosures, and records recur, but the rule, actor, purpose, jurisdiction, risk, and effective date can change what is valid.
2. **PURPOSE · INPUT · OUTPUT · OWNER.** Use synthetic records to bind each component to authority, evidence, required fields, accountable role, review gate, version, effective period, and prohibited reuse.
3. **APPROVE · CONSENT · DISCLOSE · RECORD.** Compose only compatible components. Preserve order, dependencies, decisions, signatures, provenance, retention, correction, withdrawal, and human review.
4. **ACTOR · USE · PLACE · CONSEQUENCE.** When a declared condition changes, recompute applicability, required evidence, permissions, language, review, downstream records, and every affected component.
5. **STALE · CONFLICT · MISSING · REVOKED.** Fail closed on expired authority, incompatible versions, missing evidence, revoked consent, broken dependencies, or context outside the component’s declared scope.
6. **REUSE THE CONTROL—NOT THE ASSUMPTION.** Regulated components are reusable only when context, evidence, authority, version, and invalidation travel with them.

## Rights, privacy, security, and accessibility

Original deterministic typography and layout by Rayven-Nikkita Collins / RN Builds. No third-party footage, images, music, voice, logos, or personal likenesses are used. All requirements, approvals, consent, evidence, disclosures, records, contexts, and invalidations described are synthetic. No credentials, secrets, personal data, customer content, regulated records, signatures, or real operational decisions are included. The film demonstrates a bounded component model and does not establish legal applicability, valid consent, regulatory compliance, evidentiary sufficiency, authorization, safety, or fitness for a real workflow.

The silent-first composition does not require hearing. High-contrast on-screen text carries the complete narrative. The poster and ordered six-frame contact sheet provide static alternatives. The transcript above is the caption-equivalent record.
