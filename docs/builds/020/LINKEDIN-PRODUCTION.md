# Build 020 LinkedIn production record

## Purpose

This 18-second, silent-first 4:5 film explains the Decision Evidence Ledger without implying that provenance alone proves truth, causation, legal authority, compliance, or substantive correctness.

## Storyboard and full on-screen transcript

Each scene lasts 3 seconds at 24 fps.

1. **BUILD 020 — A DECISION IS NOT A SNAPSHOT.** Accountability requires the actor, authority, time, evidence, alternatives, acknowledgement, result, and corrections.
2. **RECONSTRUCT — LINK THE COMPLETE RECORD.** Open one decision into a temporal chain instead of leaving its rationale scattered.
3. **EVIDENCE — REMOVAL MUST EXPOSE THE GAP.** A ledger should show when supporting evidence is missing—not silently preserve a false sense of completeness.
4. **CORRECT — APPEND. SUPERSEDE. NEVER ERASE.** Corrections preserve the earlier record, the change, its author, its time, and its reason.
5. **BOUNDARY — PROVENANCE ≠ PROOF.** A complete sequence does not prove truth, causation, legal authority, compliance, or a correct decision.
6. **SAVE THIS — KEEP THE WHO · WHAT · WHEN · WHY.** An accountable decision remains reconstructable after the moment has passed.

Final proposition: **AN ACCOUNTABLE DECISION REMAINS RECONSTRUCTABLE AFTER THE MOMENT HAS PASSED.**
