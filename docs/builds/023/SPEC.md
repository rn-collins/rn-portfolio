# Build 023 specification

## Frozen canon

- Title: Consent & Data Rights Manager
- Job: Let people understand and control consent, access, correction, deletion, retention and downstream use.
- B-Web: A static checkbox becomes a living rights and permission surface.
- Uses: 010 and 020
- Creates: cap:023

## Derived implementation

The functional build separates permission by specific purpose, preserves append-only grant and withdrawal history, verifies chronological state continuity, exposes retention and downstream-use terms, and names routes for access, correction, deletion, withdrawal, retention, and reuse questions.

This is a product-control demonstration, not legal advice or a determination of consent validity, applicable law, lawful basis, identity, authorization, deadlines, exemptions, deletion duties, or whether a legal right applies. Withdrawal changes future processing that relies on that permission; it does not erase historical facts or independently resolve another lawful basis, a governing exception, or retention duty.

Fixtures are synthetic and client-local. Do not enter real personal data.
