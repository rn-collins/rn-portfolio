## On-screen transcript / storyboard

1. **Bundled consent hides the deal.** A single yes cannot show purpose, data, recipient, retention, reuse, or withdrawal.
2. **Separate every purpose.** Each purpose needs its own scope, permission state, evidence, and decision history.
3. **Events must tell the truth.** Grant and withdrawal records must form a valid, chronological, append-only state chain.
4. **Withdrawal changes what happens next.** It stops future consent-based processing for that purpose—without erasing prior facts or overriding lawful obligations.
5. **Route every rights request.** Access, correction, deletion, retention, withdrawal, and downstream-use questions need an owner, deadline, status, and escalation path.
6. **Control requires a living record.** Consent is specific, changeable, evidenced, and never the only possible legal basis.
