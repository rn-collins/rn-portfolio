## Storyboard and on-screen transcript

The film is silent-first. All meaning is present as on-screen text; these six scenes run for three seconds each.

1. **A PREFERENCE IS NOT A PERMANENT TRAIT.** What helps or overwhelms a person can change with setting, goal, health, energy, company, timing, access, and prior response.
2. **PREFERENCE · SENSITIVITY · GOAL · RESPONSE.** Use synthetic browser-local records. Collect only what the person chooses, name purpose and expiry, preserve source and uncertainty, and offer correction, deletion, and export.
3. **SETTING · TIME · SUPPORT · CONSTRAINT.** Bind each response to the conditions in which it occurred. Keep contradictions, unknowns, changes, and one-off experiences instead of flattening them into a fixed profile.
4. **REORGANIZE—DO NOT DIAGNOSE.** When setting, goals, sensitivities, or responses change, reorder the visible constellation without inferring identity, disability, health status, intent, or what will work next.
5. **VIEW · CORRECT · WITHDRAW · EXIT.** Let the person inspect every field, restrict reuse and sharing, revoke consent, delete local state, carry a portable record, and choose no recommendation.
6. **CONTEXT BELONGS TO THE PERSON.** Personal context becomes useful when it stays private, correctable, situational, and owned by the person it describes.

## Rights, privacy, security, and accessibility

Original deterministic typography and layout by Rayven-Nikkita Collins / RN Builds. No third-party footage, images, music, voice, logos, or personal likenesses are used. All people, settings, preferences, sensitivities, goals, responses, health references, and context records described are synthetic and browser-local in the demonstrated model. No real identity, disability, health, sensory, location, relationship, customer, or behavioral records are included. The film does not diagnose, infer protected traits or intent, predict what will work, establish a complete profile, or authorize sharing, recommendation, or consequential use.

The silent-first composition does not require hearing. High-contrast on-screen text carries the complete narrative. The poster and ordered six-frame contact sheet provide static alternatives. The transcript above is the caption-equivalent record.
