# Build 015 — Repeated Legal Task → Product Engine

## Job
Find which recurring supervised legal tasks can safely become reusable products or systems.

## Architecture
Build 015 inherits service-to-software decomposition from 005, product-form selection from 006, and legal judgment allocation from 014. It scores repetition, consistency, reviewability, reuse value, judgment intensity, confidentiality risk, and matter specificity. It returns one of four dispositions: product candidate, internal system only, supervised service, or do not productize.

## Boundary
The public demonstration is educational product architecture. It is not legal advice, an ethics opinion, a UPL determination, authorization to provide legal services, or a substitute for competent supervision and jurisdiction-specific analysis.
