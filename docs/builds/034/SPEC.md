# Build 034 — AI Failure & Incident Reconstruction Lab

## Frozen canon
- Job: Reconstruct what happened when an AI-assisted system failed and test prevention or recovery.
- B-Web: A timeline and dependency graph replay the incident and alternate rollback points.
- Uses: 018, 019, 020, 029, 032. Creates: cap:034.

## Integrity model — engine 034.2.0

This is a synthetic chronology and counterfactual-rule exercise, not a forensic conclusion. Every event has a unique ID, timezone-explicit timestamp, actor, bounded statement, dependencies, confidence, and a redacted evidence object with unique identity, source, integrity-digest field, observed/collected timestamps, clock identity/uncertainty, scope, and conflicts. IDs/digests demonstrate schema only, not authenticity.

Missing/duplicate evidence, missing integrity/scope, invalid clock chronology, future collection, unresolved conflicts, unredacted material, missing dependencies, cycles, dependency/time contradictions, and incomplete lineage block reconstruction. Lineage explicitly covers Builds 018, 019, 020, 029, and 032.

Chronology and declared dependencies establish sequence/correlation only. They do not prove root cause, fault, foreseeability, responsibility, intent, admission, or legal causation. Uncertainty is always surfaced.

Affected-person/impact records require impact, affected group, verification, and support owner. Counterfactual prevention/containment/recovery controls require a target event, evidence, feasibility, and a completed test. The only result is `MODELED DIFFERENCE` or `NO MODELED CHANGE`; neither proves prevention, containment, recovery, or rollback efficacy.

Export preserves ordered original and derived state, evidence and clock assumptions, impacts, lineage, engine version, exact replay input, privacy/redaction boundary, and limitations. Do not paste real personal, confidential, privileged, security-sensitive, or production incident evidence.

A/B provide native reversible keyboard controls, live status, described graph, focus visibility, 320px layout, and reduced-motion handling. Real incident-response/forensic review, evidence custody verification, affected-person notice/support, production rollback testing, legal/security review, authenticated browser, physical-device/manual accessibility, hosted CI, and LinkedIn verification remain external gates.
