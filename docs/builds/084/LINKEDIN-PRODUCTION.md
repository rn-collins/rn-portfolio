## LinkedIn launch post
A product can make its final form visible while hiding the people, knowledge, stewardship, rights, labor, capital, and agreements that made it possible.

Build 084 makes that structure inspectable.

The Benefit-Sharing Intelligence System traces a fixed fictional value chain from contribution to capture, then holds stated commitments beside what the record actually shows: met, partial, unmet, or still unknown.

It does not decide what is fair. It does not establish ownership, rights, consent, breach, value, compensation, or entitlement. It creates the record a qualified human would need before making any of those judgments.
