# Build 088 — Creator Relationship & Opportunity Intelligence System

Canonical public and B title: **Your Network Is Not a Contact List**. Build 088 uses 011, 020, and 061 and creates `cap:088`. Its artifacts are `Relationship Memory` and `Opportunity Pipeline`. The visual mechanism turns names into an active opportunity-and-relationship graph.

The deterministic public tool admits five exact fictional records: an invited scoping path, a due promise, limited permission, private context, and mismatched proof. Gate precedence is private context, open commitments, permission, proof relevance, then human conflict review. No relationship score or semantic fit inference is used.

All people, organizations, communications, opportunities and outcomes are fictional. The tool does not scrape, send messages, update a CRM, schedule meetings, or evaluate a person’s worth. Relationship context is not consent; an introduction is not permission to pitch; a public build is not proof of fit; a proposal simulation is not approval or a promise of work. Human privacy, conflict, accuracy, permission and relationship judgment remain required.

A provides a relationship check, proposal-draft simulation and minimized deterministic JSON export. B begins closed with `REVEAL RELATIONSHIP CONTEXT`, then applies permission and proof gates to a keyboard-readable path. Both preserve native controls, live status, visible focus, reduced motion, print disclosure and 320px containment.
