# Build 016 — Legal Workflow Mapping System

Maps actors, documents, authorities, handoffs, deadlines, decisions, risks, and records across legal work. It inherits structural validity from Build 010 and legal judgment allocation from Build 014. A handoff is complete only when its payload, sender, receiver, trigger, deadline, acceptance test, authority, record, and escalation path are explicit.

Boundary: educational workflow architecture—not legal advice, a professional-responsibility conclusion, or jurisdiction-specific compliance.
