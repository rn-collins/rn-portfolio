# Build 060 — Catch the IP You Create Before It Disappears Into the Workday

## Canon

- B-Web: A workday emits assets into an evolving IP map; uncaptured items disappear or become public.

## Contract
Engine `060.1.0` routes fixed synthetic artifact observations across creator assertion, custody, employment/contract assignment, confidentiality, disclosure/publication, evidence, jurisdiction, title, protectability, authorship/inventorship, registration and counsel route. Those fields remain distinct. Unknown, missing, public-disclosure and assignment-conflict states fail closed to a gap, hold or counsel conflict route.

Exact inherited contracts: `011.1.0`, `020.2.0`, `027.2.0`, `029.2.0`, and `057.1.0`, preserving their identity, append-correction, operational ownership, exact-change and recorded-provenance limits.

Recursive exact validation is serialization-free and no-throw for malformed, duplicate, reordered, extra, non-finite, BigInt, proxy/accessor and serialization-hook inputs. Export is state-only.

## Boundaries
All artifacts and records are synthetic. Not legal advice or a determination of IP rights, title, ownership, authorship, inventorship, protectability, confidentiality, trade-secret status, assignment, validity, enforceability, infringement, registration, filing deadline, loss of rights, prior art, license, third-party rights, value or commercial significance. Routing to counsel is not a filing decision or authorization. Real use requires contemporaneous evidence preservation, employment/contract and contributor review, disclosure/confidentiality controls, jurisdiction-specific qualified counsel and explicit deadline management.
