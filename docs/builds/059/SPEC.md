## Contract
Engine `059.1.0` uses only the explicitly fictional “In re Synthetic Harbor” citation and fictional court. Existence, exact quotation, proposition, jurisdiction/court/date/version, current status and treatment remain separate gates. Unknown or conflict states require competent human legal research; a failed gate produces DO NOT RELY. Even all recorded passes say DO NOT RELY until independently verified.

Inherited contracts are exact: `028.2.0` / `transparent-ranking-v2` / `derived-concept-map-v2`; `029.2.0`; and claim trace `058.1.0`. Relevance/classification, registered impact and evidence linkage do not prove truth, authority, quality, applicability, sufficiency, causation, materiality, legal effect, completeness, admissibility, validity, consensus or endorsement.

Recursive exact validation is serialization-free and no-throw for malformed, duplicate, reordered, extra, non-finite, BigInt, proxy/accessor and serialization-hook inputs. Export is state-only.

## Boundaries
Not legal research or advice, a citator, or proof of existence, currentness, good law, precedential value, proposition support, jurisdiction, quote accuracy, court-record completeness, authenticity, admissibility or authorization to cite or rely. No live court, docket, opinion, statute, rule, database or treatment history is searched. Real use requires competent current legal research in authoritative sources, exact opinion/context review, jurisdiction and hierarchy analysis, current citator/treatment review, procedural-history review and lawyer judgment.
