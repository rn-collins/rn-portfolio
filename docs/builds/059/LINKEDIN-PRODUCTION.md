## Boundaries

All citation and gate records are synthetic. The package performs no legal research, citator search, source retrieval, quotation verification, jurisdiction analysis, treatment analysis, or legal advice. It cannot establish any real authority's existence, quote, currentness, good-law status, precedential or persuasive value, proposition support, jurisdictional applicability, treatment, completeness, reliability, citator coverage, or fitness for reliance. A synthetic pass/fail gate is a teaching state, not a legal conclusion. Real reliance requires primary-source retrieval, exact quotation/locator review, full subsequent-history and citator research across appropriate services, jurisdiction-specific analysis, independent updating, and competent licensed counsel.
