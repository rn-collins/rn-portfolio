## Alt text

An 18-second silent, text-led animation on a warm cream background introduces RN Build 071. A dark teal bar marks a fictional legal status as open, then asks whether someone can actually reach it. Six modular rows appear for eligibility, clinician or handoff, geography and supply, cost and coverage, disability and language, and digital access and transport. Several rows remain unresolved or blocked. The final screen reads “Legal does not mean accessible” and contrasts the open legal status with unresolved, constrained, unknown, and blocked practical-access conditions. It ends by saying to track the distance between legal and reachable and states that the synthetic piece provides no medical or legal advice and makes no access, safety, or effectiveness claim. There is no audio.
