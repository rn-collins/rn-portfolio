# Build 071 — Medical Cannabis Access Intelligence Dashboard

`071-A` combines wholly fictional policy, eligibility-rule, clinician-capacity, geography/travel, supply/product-availability, cost/payment, accommodation, consent/privacy, evidence-date, unknown, barrier, and audit signals without converting them into an access decision. `071-B` reveals practical barriers between a fictional legal-status record and care while preserving every unresolved boundary.

## Admission and safety

Only the exact five frozen fixtures are admitted. Unknown keys, mutations, getters, proxies, real-authority or real-program flags, health-data claims, lineage drift, malformed values, and unrecognized scenarios fail closed to `INVALID` without throwing. Every jurisdiction, program, persona, policy, rule, clinician, product, supply, cost, and barrier record is fictional. The engine provides no diagnosis, treatment, dosage, product recommendation, clinician verification, legal advice, current-law or compliance conclusion, eligibility or access determination, safety or effectiveness claim, consent-validity decision, privacy-compliance conclusion, or outcome prediction.

## Deterministic states

1. `POLICY-RECORD-RULE-UNKNOWN` → `POLICY SIGNAL ONLY`
2. `RULE-MATCH-CLINICIAN-GAP` → `CLINICIAN BARRIER SIGNAL`
3. `CLINICIAN-RECORDED-TRAVEL-BARRIER` → `GEOGRAPHY BARRIER SIGNAL`
4. `SUPPLY-COST-CONSTRAINTS` → `SUPPLY AND COST SIGNAL`
5. `ACCOMMODATION-PRIVACY-REVIEW` → `ACCOMMODATION REVIEW SIGNAL`

Every result keeps the eight access dimensions, dated evidence, barriers, unknowns, non-claims, and an ordered 11-stage audit separate. A recorded policy, synthetic rule match, directory snapshot, inventory snapshot, or price snapshot never means that a person is eligible, a clinician is verified, a product is suitable, or care is accessible.
