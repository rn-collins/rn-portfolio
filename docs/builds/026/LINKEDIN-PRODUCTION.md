## On-screen transcript / storyboard

1. **A clean table can hide a messy history.** Completeness on screen does not prove the records are accurate, representative, current, or fit for this decision.
2. **Trace where every field came from.** Keep source, collection method, transformation, timestamp, owner, and permitted use inspectable.
3. **Test the failure modes.** Missingness, duplication, conflict, drift, outliers, and label uncertainty must remain separate signals.
4. **Ask who is missing.** Coverage can look large while systematically excluding people, places, outcomes, or hard-to-record cases.
5. **Bound the claim and the use.** Name what the data can support, what it cannot support, the accountable reviewer, and the next verification action.
6. **Do not clean away the warning.** Messy data can support a decision only when its limits remain visible.
