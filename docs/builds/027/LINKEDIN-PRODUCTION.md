## On-screen transcript / storyboard

1. **Posting is not ownership.** A platform can distribute the work without giving you control of the audience, archive, identity, or future access.
2. **Name what you actually own.** Track source files, authorship, licenses, collaborators, permissions, registrations, and every reused component.
3. **Build a direct relationship.** Consent-based email, portable contacts, preference history, and clear unsubscribe routes survive an algorithm change.
4. **Keep the canonical copy.** Use your domain, durable archive, structured metadata, exports, backups, and migration paths as the source of truth.
5. **Let platforms be channels.** Syndicate outward, measure dependencies, preserve attribution, and keep a tested exit for every critical service.
6. **Reach is not control.** Platform reach is rented. Your ownership stack must survive the platform.
