# Build 041 — Who Owns the AI Work?

Frozen canon: show who is responsible for each AI tool, decision, vendor and workflow inside an organization. B-Web: an organizational topology reveals orphaned accountability and changes under reassignment. Uses 011, 012, 013, 020; creates cap:041. Build 042 directly consumes the capability.

Engine 041.1.0 inspects a bounded synthetic inventory for unique records, named accountable and operational owners, decision authority, evidence identity, review date and expiry. Reassignment changes declared topology and coverage without mutating a real organization.

OWNED means only that every declared synthetic record satisfies the visible schema. It does not establish legal responsibility, actual authority, effective oversight, vendor control, discovery completeness or organizational adoption.
