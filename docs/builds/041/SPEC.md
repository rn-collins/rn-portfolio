# Build 041 — Who Owns the AI Work?

Frozen canon: show who is responsible for each AI tool, decision, vendor and workflow inside an organization. B-Web: an organizational topology reveals orphaned accountability and changes under reassignment. Uses 011, 012, 013, 020; creates cap:041. Build 042 directly consumes the capability.

Engine 041.2.0 inspects a bounded synthetic inventory for unique record and evidence identities, exact fixture identity/kind/evidence bindings, distinct accountable owner, operational owner, and decision authority, and real calendar review/expiry dates current at the declared evaluation date. Reassignment changes declared topology and coverage without mutating a real organization. Conflicted or mismatched records cannot count toward coverage.

OWNED means only that every declared synthetic record satisfies the visible schema and fixture contract. It does not establish legal responsibility, actual authority, effective oversight, vendor control, discovery completeness or organizational adoption.
