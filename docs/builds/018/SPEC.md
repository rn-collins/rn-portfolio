# Build 018 — Institutional Friction Detector

## Job

Find bottlenecks, dead ends, repeated loops, duplicate demands, and avoidable burden inside institutional processes.

## Functional build

`018-A` maps organizations, accountable transfers, and experienced process steps. The deterministic `018.1.0` engine flags five observable friction classes, reports the inherited Build 016 workflow and Build 017 handoff states, ranks repairs, simulates a bounded repair, resets, restores, and exports a local JSON record.

## Paired visual

`018-B` turns one institutional path into four interactive states—submit, wait, repeat, decide—so the cost hidden inside an official diagram becomes visible.

## Lineage

- Uses `016`: accountable workflow nodes and nine-field handoff records.
- Uses `017`: cross-organization responsibility, permission, evidence, incident, record, and recourse controls.
- Creates `cap:018`: institutional process-friction findings and prioritized repair order.
- Future consumers: `019`, `034`, `048`, and `093`.

## Evidence boundary

OMB service-delivery and administrative-burden guidance supports treating time, learning, compliance, access, and psychological costs as design concerns. The five-signal detector and numeric score are transparent product heuristics. They do not prove causation, quantify lived burden completely, determine legal compliance, or replace research with affected people.
