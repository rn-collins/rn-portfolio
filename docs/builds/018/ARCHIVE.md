# Build 018 archive

Build 018 turns an institutional flowchart into an inspectable experienced path. It preserves the official actors and transfers inherited from Builds 016 and 017 while adding time, repetition, duplicate evidence, exit, ownership, and recourse signals.

The deliberately flawed fixture contains a 96-hour wait against a 48-hour target, three review loops, and a duplicate evidence demand. The bounded repair reuses the already accepted evidence, reduces the observed wait, and removes the loop without changing eligibility or decision authority.

The public demonstration is local-only and uses synthetic content. It is not a compliance audit, eligibility decision, causal model, or complete burden study. Direct research with affected people remains necessary.
