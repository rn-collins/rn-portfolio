# Build 051 — Teach Me the Cannabis Part I Actually Need

## Canon

- Job: Build an adaptive cannabis learning path based on question, experience, misconceptions, jurisdiction and risk.
- B-Web: A curriculum graph rearranges as learner state and context change.
- Uses: 002, 028, 030.
- Creates: `cap:051`.
- Direct consumers: 052, 078, 097.

## Product and contract

Version A routes one exact-bound synthetic learner among foundational, topic, misconception, and high-risk safety steps. Version B shows how three synthetic contexts produce different starting paths. Engine `051.1.0` accepts only a six-key named fixture, closed enums, case-exact unique lists, and `UNSPECIFIED` jurisdiction. It routes topics; it does not answer personal medical or legal questions.

High-risk flags pause general education and expose an authoritative/professional escalation message. A law question always produces `CHECK CURRENT LAW`; no jurisdiction-specific proposition is bundled into the deterministic fixture. Export is atomic and state-only.

## Boundaries

Not medical or legal advice, diagnosis, treatment, product recommendation, dosing guidance, safety clearance, effectiveness finding, or a statement of current law. It does not tell anyone whether to use cannabis. Real use requires current primary sources plus clinical, public-health, cannabis, legal, youth/pregnancy, disability/lived-experience, privacy, safety, and accessibility review.
