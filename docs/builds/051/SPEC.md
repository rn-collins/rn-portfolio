# Build 051 — Teach Me the Cannabis Part I Actually Need

## Canon

- Job: Build an adaptive cannabis learning path based on question, experience, misconceptions, jurisdiction and risk.
- B-Web: A curriculum graph rearranges as learner state and context change.
- Uses: 002, 028, 030.
- Creates: `cap:051`.
- Direct consumers: 052, 078, 097.

## Product and contract

Version A routes one exact-bound synthetic learner among foundational, topic, misconception, and high-risk safety steps. Version B shows how three synthetic contexts produce different starting paths. Engine `051.2.0` accepts only an eight-key named fixture, closed enums, case-exact unique lists, `UNSPECIFIED` jurisdiction, a strict calendar evaluation date, and the exact ordered primary-source registry derived from `data/build-051-safety-sources-v1.json`. Missing, duplicate, reordered, altered, stale (more than 366 days), or future-checked records fail closed. A record marked `FUTURE` is accepted only before its exact effective date; the 2026-11-12 federal-hemp transition therefore cannot remain labeled future on or after that date.

High-risk flags take precedence, pause general education, and expose authoritative/professional escalation messages. A law question without a risk flag produces `CHECK CURRENT LAW`; no jurisdiction-specific proposition is bundled into the deterministic fixture. Source checks are dated snapshots, not live updates or proof that a proposition remains current. Export is atomic and state-only and includes the validated registry and evaluation date.

## Boundaries

Not medical or legal advice, diagnosis, treatment, product recommendation, dosing guidance, safety clearance, effectiveness finding, or a statement of current law. It does not tell anyone whether to use cannabis. Real use requires freshly checked current primary sources plus clinical, public-health, cannabis, legal, youth/pregnancy, disability/lived-experience, privacy, safety, and accessibility review.
