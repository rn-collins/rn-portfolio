## On-screen transcript / storyboard

1. **Stored is not found.** A fact can exist in the archive and still fail the person who needs it at the moment of decision.
2. **Start with the question.** Model the language people actually use, the task they are doing, and the consequence of retrieving the wrong thing.
3. **Give knowledge more than a title.** Stable identity, relationships, provenance, dates, audiences, and status make retrieval inspectable.
4. **Rank relevance with authority.** Similarity alone is not enough. Preserve source quality, freshness, scope, contradiction, and uncertainty.
5. **Test what people can actually find.** Use known questions, missed results, false confidence, correction paths, and human feedback to measure the system.
6. **Finding is part of knowing.** Knowledge you cannot reliably find is knowledge you cannot reliably use.
