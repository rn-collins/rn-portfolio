## Technical contract

Inputs are eight declared control conditions plus workflow and consequence context. Each dimension maps to 0, 1, or 2. The sum is labeled Strong at 13–16, Partial at 8–12, and Weak at 0–7. All sub-2 dimensions create repair candidates; high consequence promotes evidence, authority, and timing. These values, weights, thresholds, and ordering are RN product heuristics—not legal or source-mandated rules.

The browser renders the assessment and protocol locally. Export occurs only on explicit request. A result never authorizes deployment or proves oversight works in practice.
