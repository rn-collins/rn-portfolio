# Build 019 LinkedIn production record

## Purpose

This 18-second, silent-first 4:5 film explains the Consequence-Aware Escalation Router without implying that notification volume, a score, or an available person is sufficient control.

## Storyboard and full on-screen transcript

Each scene lasts 3 seconds at 24 fps.

1. **BUILD 019 — 100 ALERTS ≠ 100 INCIDENTS.** Repeated signals can describe one consequential event—not one hundred separate interruptions.
2. **CORRELATE — GROUP THE EVIDENCE FIRST.** Shared keys collapse repeated signals while preserving every source and state.
3. **ASSESS — SCORE CONSEQUENCE TRANSPARENTLY.** Severity, scale, rights, reversibility, evidence, and inherited friction remain inspectable.
4. **ROUTE — AUTHORITY BEFORE AVAILABILITY.** Notify only an authorized owner through a compatible channel, with the reason visible.
5. **ACKNOWLEDGE — MISSED CLOCK → NAMED BACKUP.** A recorded acknowledgement stops the timeout; otherwise a validated backup receives the chain.
6. **SAVE THIS — AUTHORITY · EVIDENCE · TIME · RECOURSE.** Escalation is an accountable chain—not a louder notification.

Final proposition: **ESCALATION IS A CHAIN OF AUTHORITY, EVIDENCE, TIME, AND RECOURSE.**
