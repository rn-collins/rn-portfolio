# Build 019 — Consequence-Aware Escalation Router

## Job

Route meaningful events to the right person, urgency, channel, and next step without hiding suppression, authority, or timeout logic.

## Functional build

`019-A` correlates repeated events, computes a transparent consequence score, checks required authority and compatible channels, starts an acknowledgement clock, reroutes to a named backup, exposes suppressed events, and exports a local JSON record. The deterministic `019.1.0` engine directly executes the Build 012 consequence-control, Build 017 regulated-handoff, and Build 018 institutional-friction assessments.

## Paired visual

`019-B` moves from 100 signals to one correlated incident, one authorized owner, and one visible acknowledgement timer.

## Evidence boundary

NIST incident-response guidance supports defined roles, event analysis, magnitude validation, prioritization, communication, response, and recovery. CISA situational-awareness guidance warns against alarm inundation and fatigue. The score, thresholds, correlation key, timeout, and route selection are transparent product heuristics—not universal incident, legal, clinical, employment, safety, or compliance rules.

## Release truth

The implementation, tests, archives, privacy/freshness records, and public A/B rooms are committed independently from deployment, browser, GitHub-hosted CI, and LinkedIn/media certification evidence.
