# Build 083 — Work-to-Content Capture Engine

## Canonical contract

Build 083 uses Builds 024, 057, 060, and 080 and creates `cap:083`. Its exact artifacts are `Confidentiality Filter` and `Content Opportunity Extractor`.

083-A is **Work-to-Content Capture Engine**: it extracts publishable insight, proof of work, methods, visuals, and teaching material from real work without exposing confidential information. Registry title: **Turn Real Work Into Public Proof Without Leaking the Work**. Job: **Extract publishable insight, methods, visuals and proof while protecting confidential material.** 083-B is **Your Work Is Already Full of Content**: a completed project separates into safe public artifacts. On the web, a work artifact passes through confidentiality and rights filters into safe derivatives.

## Frozen synthetic fixture

The public demonstration admits exactly five fictional work packets in fixed order: Cedar, Harbor, Lattice, Mesa, and Orbit. Each contains exactly six invented fragments. Two are `PUBLIC-SYNTHETIC` with `OWNED-SYNTHETIC` fixture rights and may supply derivative IDs. Four are blocked: `CONFIDENTIAL-SYNTHETIC`, `THIRD-PARTY-RESTRICTED`, `PERSONAL-SYNTHETIC`, and `INTERNAL-SYNTHETIC`. Blocked fragments contain only conspicuous synthetic placeholders—not real secrets, personal information, client material, patient material, third-party work, or privileged content.

The result exposes only the blocked fragment ID, classification, and reason; it never reproduces blocked content. Each packet produces exactly four fixed draft types—insight, method, visual, and teaching material—sourced solely from the two admitted fragment IDs. Every draft retains source links, removed-detail categories, limitations, human rights review, human confidentiality review, and `DRAFT-HUMAN-APPROVAL-REQUIRED` status.

`OWNED-SYNTHETIC` is a fixture state, not real ownership or license clearance. The engine cannot determine confidentiality, privilege, privacy, consent, copyright, trade-secret status, contractual restrictions, accuracy, context, reputational risk, or publication safety. It never publishes.

Only the five byte-for-byte frozen presets are admitted. Additional, mutated, real, secret-bearing, rights-bearing, hostile, or unrecognized input returns `INVALID`, no packet summary, no admitted IDs, no blocked metadata, and no derivatives. There is no free text, upload, network dependency, document parser, automated redaction, model inference, or fallback.

## UI and export contract

The payload contains version `083.1.0`, exact lineage, exact artifacts, full admission block, input, result, and replay data. The exported input contains only the fixed synthetic fixture placeholders; real restricted material is never admitted.

083-B starts closed and uses `FILTER WORK ARTIFACT`; its live status says `Confidential work separated into reviewable public derivatives`. The transformation must show that four classes were blocked, without rendering their content, and keep every derivative in human-review draft status.

Both routes require semantic native controls, visible keyboard focus, live status, reduced-motion support, print support, and no horizontal document overflow at 320 CSS pixels. Color is never the only carrier of classification, blocked status, rights state, or review requirement.

## Confidentiality, rights, privacy, and publication boundaries

Every project, fragment, source, method, lesson, restriction, person, organization, and derivative is fictional. Fixture classification is not a legal, contractual, ethical, or security determination. The tool does not provide legal advice, privilege review, confidentiality review, privacy review, copyright or license analysis, consent verification, trade-secret analysis, professional-duty review, fact-checking, or editorial approval.

No real client, legal matter, patient, research participant, employer, partner, source, community, organization, or third-party material may be entered into this demonstration. No output is approved or safe to disclose, reuse, distribute, post, teach, market, or publish. Qualified authorized humans must review original obligations and every final artifact.
