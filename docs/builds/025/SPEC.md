## Derived implementation

025-A tests a synthetic use across people, data, workflow, controls, and evidence. Version 025.2.0 treats a score as display input, not proof: every positive score needs a specific evidence statement, locatable dated records, a reviewer, an accountable owner, the owner's authority, and a validation method. Full readiness cannot retain a stated gap.

Builds 012, 013, and 021 are represented by separate traceable assessment records. A checkbox or a layer-strengthening action cannot silently close an inherited gate. One record per inherited build must identify the assessment, engine version, outcome, review date, closure state, and unresolved gaps.

A bounded pilot additionally requires explicit data and action exclusions, approval and expiration records, success and stop measures, incident routing, and rollback. All five layers must be fully validated before the highest state is possible.

025-B reveals why a persuasive demonstration is not organizational readiness.

The question, thesis, fixture, rubric, thresholds, public explanation, and “Readiness Has Layers” label are derived. READY FOR BOUNDED PILOT is limited to the synthetic boundary; it is not production, procurement, compliance, legal, regulatory, or safety authorization.
