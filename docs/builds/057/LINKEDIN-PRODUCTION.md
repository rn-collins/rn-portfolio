The interface exposes recorded claims and links; it does not independently verify them. Presence, absence, timestamps, metadata, stylistic features, or an AI-use assertion are not AI detection. Real use requires primary records plus competent legal/IP, licensing, archives/records, provenance, digital-forensics, security, privacy, editorial, creator/labor, ethics, and accessibility review.
