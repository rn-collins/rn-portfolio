## Contract
Engine `057.1.0` traces exact fixed synthetic nodes and ordered events. States remove a recorded source assertion, remove a recorded rights assertion, or reorder version events. A graph edge means only that the fixture records an assertion.

Inherited contracts are exact: Build 020 `020.2.0` requires linked record fields and append-only corrections; provenance is not truth, sequence is not causation, and completeness is not correctness or lawfulness. Build 023 `023.2.0` preserves an append-only consent/rights chain but product control is not consent validity, lawful basis, identity, authorization, deletion or legal rights. Build 029 `029.2.0` traces exact registered change only, not causation, materiality, legal effect or completeness.

Exact recursive structural validation avoids serialization. Missing, duplicate, reordered, extra, non-finite, BigInt, proxy/accessor or serialization-hook input fails closed without throwing. Export is atomic and state-only.

## Boundaries
No real artifact, source, person, tool, AI system, right, license, version or event is represented. Recorded provenance is not verified truth, accuracy, originality, authorship, ownership, permission, license validity, complete AI disclosure, identity, attribution, authenticity, chronology, chain of custody, admissibility or evidentiary weight. Not legal advice or a copyright, title, authorization or authenticity determination. Real use requires source verification, identity/role validation, append-only correction governance, cryptographic/tamper controls where appropriate, rights/legal review, privacy/security/accessibility review, and an explicit completeness standard.
