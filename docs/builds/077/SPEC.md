# Build 077 — Regulatory Change → Consequence Engine

## Canonical contract

Build 077 uses Builds 016, 017, 022, 029, 058, 059, 069, and 076 and creates `cap:077`. Its exact artifacts are `Change Impact Graph` and `Consequence Rules`.

077-A is **Regulatory Change → Consequence Engine**: it translates a legal or policy change into affected actors, obligations, timelines, risks, opportunities, and unresolved questions. Registry title: **What Does This Change Actually Do?** Job: **Translate a legal or policy change into affected actors, obligations, deadlines, risks and systems.** 077-B is **What Does This Change Actually Do?**: a regulatory notice sends consequence lines through an ecosystem. On the web, a clause or change sends consequence lines through an actor and workflow graph.

## Frozen synthetic fixture

The engine admits exactly five fictional changes in fixed order: Cedar, Harbor, Lattice, Mesa, and Orbit. Each contains one fixed fictional source with title, locator, verbatim synthetic clause, issue date, effective date or explicit null, as-of date, and synthetic-source status. Each preserves fixed actors, obligations, deadlines, dependencies, conditional risks with unassessed severity, conditional opportunities, and unresolved questions.

Orbit deliberately has no effective date, activation owner, or derivable deadline. Harbor and Mesa retain unspecified intervals. The engine never calculates, imputes, reconciles, completes, or silently drops these unknowns. Graph edges are mechanical representations of the frozen records, not legal conclusions.

Only the five byte-for-byte frozen presets are admitted. Additional, mutated, real, authority-bearing, hostile, or unrecognized input returns `INVALID`, an empty graph, and no trace. There is no free text, network dependency, live legal feed, geolocation, randomness, inference about people or places, or fallback to an apparently similar change.

## UI and export contract

077-A uses native select label `CHANGE NOTICE` and buttons `RESTORE CEDAR CHANGE` and `EXPORT CONSEQUENCE TRACE`. The exact export filename is `synthetic-regulatory-consequence-trace.json`. The payload contains version `077.1.0`, exact lineage, exact artifacts, full admission block, input, result, and replay data.

077-B starts closed and uses `PROPAGATE CHANGE`; its live status says `One clause now reaches actors, deadlines, risks, and systems`. The transformation must retain the source clause, locator, dates, explicit unknowns, and unassessed conditional status.

Both routes require semantic native controls, visible keyboard focus, live status, reduced-motion support, print support, and no horizontal document overflow at 320 CSS pixels. Color is never the only carrier of status.

## Legal, evidence, and deadline boundaries

Every change, notice, source, clause, date, actor, obligation, deadline, dependency, risk, opportunity, and unknown is fictional. `SYNTHETIC-SOURCE-CHECKED` means only that a record passed fixture validation. It does not verify real law or facts.

The build is not current law, legal research, legal advice, or a compliance determination. It cannot determine legal effect, applicability, obligation, rights, liability, enforcement, opportunity, risk severity, outcome, or a deadline for any real actor. Dates cannot be calendared or relied upon. A qualified professional must inspect current primary authorities and facts for any real question.

## LinkedIn boundary contract

The silent-first 18-second asset may communicate only that one fictional clause can touch multiple actors and systems. It must visibly say `FICTIONAL CHANGE — NOT CURRENT LAW` and `NO LEGAL ADVICE, COMPLIANCE, DEADLINE, RIGHTS, LIABILITY, ENFORCEMENT, OR OUTCOME CLAIM`, with the same boundary in caption and alt text.

No real laws, notices, agencies, officials, citations, people, facilities, seals, flags, statistics, or current dates. No authoritative checkmarks, compliance colors, countdown timers, legal conclusions, risk scores, winners, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Acceptance

Playwright asserts exact scenario order, deterministic graph construction, field/date/unknown preservation, no imputation, hostile-input fail closure, exact export admission/artifacts/lineage, legal and deadline boundaries, 320-pixel containment, and the B-side propagation status.
