- Job: Preserve policy, market, community, clinical, legal, historical, and operational knowledge that is otherwise repeatedly lost.
- B-Web: Cycles of forgotten cannabis knowledge become a recoverable archive.
- Uses: 028, 029, 058, 063.
- Creates: `cap:064` — Cannabis Memory Taxonomy; Archive Ingestion Workflow.

## Contract
Engine `064.1.0` evaluates five exact synthetic archive fixtures. Events, policies, actors, terminology, claims, sources, corrections, rights, context, and provenance remain distinct. Superseded policy, contested claims, restricted rights, and missing context produce separate review states.

## Boundaries
No real person, patient, institution, company, agency, law, policy, study, product, event, community knowledge, diagnosis, treatment, dosage, or health outcome is represented. Archive labels do not establish truth, authority, current law, scientific or medical validity, consent, rights, ownership, cultural permission, or completeness.
