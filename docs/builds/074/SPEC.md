## Deterministic contract

Engine version `074.1.0` admits only the three exact frozen presets in `IMPLEMENTATION_INDEX_PRESETS`. Each preset contains the same five synthetic jurisdictions and seven integer scores from 0–4. The selected weight vector totals 100. The engine publishes every component score, weight, multiplication line, evidence identifier, tie-break rule, and scoring rule. Scores are weighted, divided by four, ordered descending, and tied by stable jurisdiction ID.

The illustrative enactment-led vector is 70/5/5/5/5/5/5. The implementation-led vector is 10/20/15/15/15/10/15. The balanced vector is 15/15/14/14/14/14/14. These are authored demonstration assumptions, not empirically justified policy weights.

Any missing, additional, mutated, hostile, real, authority-bearing, or unrecognized input returns `INVALID` with no scores or order. There is no imputation, free text, network dependency, randomness, current-law feed, or inference about a real person or place.

Both routes must work at 320 CSS pixels without horizontal document overflow, use visible focus, semantic headings and controls, support print, and honor reduced motion.

## Evidence and interpretation boundaries

All five jurisdictions, records, evidence IDs, scores, weights, timelines, and outcomes are synthetic. “Jurisdiction” is a neutral fixture label, not a disguised real place. A higher demo score or displayed position does not mean that a real system is authorized, operational, accessible, affordable, staffed, equitable, safe, effective, compliant, mature, or ready.

The product makes no present-law claim; supplies no legal or medical advice; does not rank, certify, recommend, fund, regulate, or select any real jurisdiction; and must not support clinical, policy, investment, enforcement, procurement, eligibility, or resource-allocation decisions. Equity and safety are disaggregated synthetic indicators, not certifications. Outcomes are synthetic recorded signals, not causal findings.
