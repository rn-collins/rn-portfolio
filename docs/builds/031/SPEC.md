## Experience

## Evidence and accessibility
The exported JSON contains canonical metadata, exact input, result, engine version, fixture label, and boundary. Both routes provide keyboard controls, visible focus through native controls, polite atomic live status, 44px actions, a 320px layout, and reduced-motion handling.

## Boundary
No real telemetry or personal data is collected, persisted, or transmitted. Event values, purposes, scoring, thresholds, and visual copy are derived. This is not legal advice or privacy certification.
