Different machine” and reveals three fictional systems named Orbit, Mosaic, and Harbor, each with different invented institutional features. The second scene reads “Open the machine” and presents an eight-part regulatory design ontology: governance, institutions, licensing, enforcement, rights, data, financing, and implementation.
