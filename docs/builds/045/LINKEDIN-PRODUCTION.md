## Storyboard and on-screen transcript

The film is silent-first. All meaning is present as on-screen text; these six scenes run for three seconds each.

1. **AVAILABILITY IS NOT ACCESS.** A program or product can exist while a person still cannot understand the rules, qualify, afford the process, reach care, find supply, or continue safely.
2. **LEARN · QUALIFY · PAY · TRAVEL.** Use synthetic people and places to trace required information, eligibility, documents, fees, appointments, distance, time, mobility, language, privacy, and support.
3. **CLINICIAN · PRODUCT · SUPPLY · FOLLOW-UP.** Keep clinical judgment human. Show availability, product and formulation constraints, pharmacy or dispensary steps, continuity, monitoring, and what remains unknown.
4. **COST · MOBILITY · ELIGIBILITY · LOCATION.** When one constraint changes, reroute the declared path and recompute time, expense, travel, required steps, barriers, alternatives, and unresolved dead ends.
5. **A ROUTE IS NOT CARE OR LEGAL ADVICE.** A modeled journey cannot verify current law, eligibility, clinical appropriateness, product safety, inventory, affordability, accessibility, or a successful outcome.
6. **MEASURE THE WHOLE JOURNEY.** Access is the whole journey through information, eligibility, cost, travel, clinicians, supply, and continuing care.

## Rights, privacy, security, and accessibility

Original deterministic typography and layout by Rayven-Nikkita Collins / RN Builds. No third-party footage, images, music, voice, logos, personal likenesses, cannabis imagery, clinical imagery, or location imagery are used. All people, places, costs, eligibility states, journeys, products, supply conditions, and care steps described are synthetic. No patient, clinician, customer, health, location, legal, or operational records are included. The film is an access-model demonstration, not medical or legal advice, and does not establish current law, eligibility, clinical appropriateness, product safety, inventory, affordability, accessibility, successful access, or health outcome.

The silent-first composition does not require hearing. High-contrast on-screen text carries the complete narrative. The poster and ordered six-frame contact sheet provide static alternatives. The transcript above is the caption-equivalent record.
