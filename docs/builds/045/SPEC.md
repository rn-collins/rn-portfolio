# Build 045 — Cannabis Care Access Journey

## Frozen canon

- Job: Show what a person must actually do, understand, pay for, travel through and overcome to access cannabis care.
- B-Web: A location and context-aware care journey reroutes under cost, mobility, eligibility, clinician and supply constraints.
- Uses: 009, 023, 028, 030, 035. Creates: `cap:045`. Direct consumers: 072, 073.

## Product contract

The functional build evaluates a wholly synthetic route under cumulative cost, travel, mobility, eligibility, clinician and supply constraints. Every result remains replayable and exportable without network activity. The visual build explains how formal access can diverge from practical access.

## Boundaries

No real law, provider, price, inventory, eligibility, diagnosis, treatment, or product recommendation is represented. Production use requires medical, legal, privacy, security, accessibility, domain and physical-device review.
