# Build 042 — Give the AI a Real Job

Frozen canon: define an AI agent’s role, tools, boundaries, review points and stop conditions. B-Web: drag responsibilities in and out and watch permissions, risk and evaluation recompute. Uses 012, 039, 040, 041; creates cap:042. Builds 043 and 092 directly consume the capability.

Engine 042.2.0 inspects a bounded synthetic role contract: identity, purpose, accountable owner, distinct allowed and prohibited work, exact approved tool grants, review points, stop conditions, evaluation criteria, and exact inherited control identities and versions. A write responsibility and its approved grant must move together, and remain blocked without the exact before-write human approval gate. Duplicate, fabricated, unapproved, or permission-escalated grants fail closed.

ROLE READY validates only the declared schema. It does not authorize a real agent, establish competence, prove safe execution, validate tool behavior, or demonstrate effective supervision.
