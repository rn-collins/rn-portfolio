# Build 012 — AI Use Consequence & Control Mapper

## Job
Classify an AI use by consequence and generate proportionate control architecture.

## Lineage
Uses Build 001 human-review controls and Build 010 structured entities, relationships, fields, and sources. Creates `cap:012`.

## Product contract
A accepts a bounded use, affected people, evidence-bearing consequence dimensions, and eleven control statements. It returns a consequence score, required-control set, coverage, inherited human-review grade, inherited skeleton state, gaps, and one of three heuristic states. B makes the same principle spatial: changing consequence visibly reconfigures control load.

## Boundary
This is a product heuristic—not legal classification, formal risk assessment, regulatory compliance, safety approval, discrimination assessment, or deployment permission. High consequence with missing controls escalates; no score executes a deployment decision.
