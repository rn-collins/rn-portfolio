## Boundaries

The decision, people, roles, evidence, sources, assumptions, alternatives, constraints, dissent, approvals, authority records, expected and observed outcomes, dates, versions, timelines, comparisons, and interpretations are synthetic. “Known then” means only recorded as available or asserted in the synthetic fixture—not true, complete, considered, understood, accepted, or actually known. “Known now” and hindsight remain separate later records and must not be projected backward. The interface cannot establish truth, causation, reasonableness, authority, completeness, correctness, legality, negligence, intent, motive, knowledge, notice, accountability, fault, standard of care, decision quality, outcome attribution, or who caused an outcome. It is not legal, governance, audit, employment, investigation, negligence, causation, accountability, or professional advice. Real use requires primary records, affected-person participation, correction and dissent paths, and competent legal, governance, audit, records/provenance, subject-matter, statistics/causal-methods, ethics, privacy, labor, and accessibility review.
