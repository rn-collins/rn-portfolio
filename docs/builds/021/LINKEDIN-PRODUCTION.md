## On-screen transcript and storyboard

1. **BUILD 021 — THE CLAIM SOUNDS CERTAIN.** Procurement still needs to know what the evidence can actually support.
2. **SEPARATE — PROMISE ≠ EVIDENCE.** Record the vendor claim separately from the artifact offered to substantiate it.
3. **TEST — SCOPE · DATE · METHOD · OWNER.** Ask whether the evidence covers this product, this use, this version, and this consequence.
4. **CONTROL — NAME THE FAILURE PATH.** A control is incomplete without an authorized owner, trigger, response, and retained record.
5. **VERDICT — SUPPORTED · PARTIAL · UNVERIFIED · CONTRADICTED.** Nonbinary findings preserve uncertainty and identify the next verification action.
6. **SAVE THIS — BUY THE EVIDENCE—NOT THE ADJECTIVE.** A defensible procurement decision keeps claim, proof, control, and recourse inspectable.
