## Derived implementation copy

Question: What would have to be true before this vendor claim could support a procurement decision?

Thesis: A vendor promise becomes decision-ready only when the exact entity, proposition, evidence, scope, consequence, control, commitment, remedy, and correction path are explicit.

The question, thesis, fixtures, labels, thresholds, and interaction language above are implementation copy derived from the canonical job and B-web. They are not separate canonical records.

## Functional contract

021-A separates three synthetic vendor claims into linked evidence, scope match, independent verification, and enforceable commitment. It preserves unresolved and conditional states, concretely displays inherited entity resolution (011), consequence controls (012), and decision evidence (020), and exports a client-local JSON x-ray.

021-B progressively breaks one promise into identity, evidence, scope, control, and contractual commitment layers.

## Boundaries

This demonstration does not endorse a vendor, conduct due diligence, validate security or compliance, provide legal advice, score value, or make a procurement decision. Real procurement requires the applicable policy, facts, contract, testing, affected groups, and competent procurement, legal, security, privacy, accessibility, finance, and domain reviewers.
