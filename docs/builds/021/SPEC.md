## Derived implementation copy

Question: What would have to be true before this vendor claim could support a procurement decision?

Thesis: A vendor promise becomes decision-ready only when the exact entity, proposition, evidence, scope, consequence, control, commitment, remedy, and correction path are explicit.

The question, thesis, fixtures, labels, thresholds, and interaction language above are implementation copy derived from the canonical job and B-web. They are not separate canonical records.

## Functional contract

021-A keeps three synthetic claims separate and evaluates ten material paths: exact identity, testable proposition, dated linked evidence, scope match, consequence assessment, demonstrated controls, independent verification, enforceable commitment, remedy, and traceable correction. The interface names every open path and exports a client-local, explicitly synthetic verification record.

Verdicts are nonbinary and deliberately narrow:

- VERIFIED FOR THIS SCOPE requires all ten recorded paths. It means only that the synthetic record is complete for the identified proposition, entity, version, and scope.
- CONDITIONAL requires identity, a testable proposition, linked evidence, and at least five closed paths. It preserves remaining conditions.
- UNRESOLVED applies when those minimum foundations are absent. Closing peripheral controls cannot cure missing identity, proposition, or evidence.

No verdict is a general endorsement, compliance or security conclusion, value judgment, vendor selection, or procurement approval. Contradictory evidence in a real review must remain in the Build 020 evidence chain and be resolved by an authorized human reviewer; a checkbox cannot erase it.

021-B progressively breaks one promise into identity, evidence, scope, control, and contractual commitment layers.

## Privacy and error boundaries

The public demonstration accepts no text or files, makes no network submission, stores nothing, and exports only synthetic fixture state. Users are told not to enter confidential information. If adapted for real procurement, validation, access control, retention, deletion, incident response, and jurisdiction-specific records duties must be designed before accepting data.

## Boundaries

This demonstration does not endorse a vendor, conduct due diligence, validate security or compliance, provide legal advice, score value, or make a procurement decision. Real procurement requires applicable policy, facts, contract, testing, affected groups, and competent procurement, legal, security, privacy, accessibility, finance, and domain reviewers.
