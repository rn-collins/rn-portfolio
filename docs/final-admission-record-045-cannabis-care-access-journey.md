## 045 — Cannabis Care Access Journey

**Public question:** If cannabis care is legal where I live, what does it actually take for a real person to get through the system?

**Public job:** Show the practical path from first question through eligibility, clinician/provider access, geography, cost, supply/product access, use/support, monitoring and recurring barriers—without pretending that legal authorization equals practical access.

**Novice first-value path:** Choose a location and a few practical circumstances such as transportation, mobility/accessibility, cost sensitivity, clinician access, caregiver support, or whether the person is new to the system. The product shows the likely journey, major barriers, what needs verification, and alternative paths where evidence supports them. No account required for the sample/public path.

**Expert depth:** jurisdiction and eligibility; clinical/provider pathway; supply/distribution; transportation; island/geospatial access; cost/time burden; disability access; workforce; stigma/cultural context; patient support; monitoring/discontinuation; source freshness and uncertainty.

**A technological ceiling:** jurisdiction-aware rules/path graph; geospatial provider/service/supply layers where reliable and lawful; travel-time/network analysis; cost/time modeling; barrier ontology; accessibility and transportation constraints; temporal freshness; scenario comparison; route/path recomputation; provenance and source versioning; uncertainty; privacy-preserving session/local mode for personal constraints; structured qualitative patient-experience inputs; optional public-data integrations. No diagnosis, individualized treatment recommendation, dose advice, or unsupported provider/product availability claims.

**What the visitor must not need to know:** cannabis law terminology, health-system design, GIS, insurance/market structure, graph theory, or clinical vocabulary.

**B-Web proposition:** “Legal” is only the first gate in an access system.

**B-Web mechanism:** a simple path marked LEGAL opens into the actual journey. The visitor changes location/island, transportation, mobility, cost, clinician availability, eligibility or supply. The path reroutes, lengthens, becomes blocked, or exposes unresolved information. A map/network view and an accessible step-by-step path remain synchronized.

**B-Web technological ceiling:** interactive geospatial/network visualization; constraint/pathfinding engine; scenario comparison; temporal data freshness; route/travel-time layers where appropriate; uncertainty display; accessible non-map linear alternative; keyboard/touch support; reduced motion.

**B-LinkedIn 4:5 MP4:** `LEGAL` appears as a clean endpoint. It moves left as eligibility, clinician, distance, cost, supply, transportation, accessibility and support gates appear one after another. Final frame: `LEGAL ≠ AVAILABLE ≠ AFFORDABLE ≠ REACHABLE ≠ USABLE.` Silent-first and readable without narration.

**Saveable payload:** `LEGAL → ELIGIBLE → CLINICIAN → REACHABLE → AFFORDABLE → AVAILABLE → USABLE → SUPPORTED`

**Evidence/limitations:** access data can become stale quickly; provider/product availability and legal requirements must carry source/date/jurisdiction metadata. The product maps access conditions and questions to verify; it does not establish legal eligibility or provide medical care.
