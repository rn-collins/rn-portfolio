# 100 Builds Replacement Tournament — Round 4

## Decision
The six challengers still standing after Round 3 are stronger public artifacts than six generic/weak incumbents. Their underlying engineering primitives remain mandatory RN Lab infrastructure; the public slot is reassigned to the consequential problem.

## Exact challenger placements

### Replace former 009 — Aloha AI Design Principles Framework
**Winner:** Place-Sensitive Technology Readiness Tool

Why: the old title is internal doctrine, while the stronger public proposition asks a reusable question: what assumptions does a technology make about the place where it will be deployed? Aloha AI can remain provenance and a worked example rather than the numbered artifact itself.

Preserved capability: design principles, localization constraints, accessibility/context modeling, implementation assumptions.

### Replace former 018 — Workflow State Machine
**Winner:** Institutional Friction Detector

Why: a state machine is valuable engineering but weak public work. Institutional friction gives the state/handoff machinery a human consequence: where ambiguity, waiting, duplicate work, authority gaps, cognitive load and invisible labor accumulate.

Preserved capability: states, transitions, wait conditions, handoffs, bottleneck detection, intervention prioritization.

### Replace former 022 — Role-Based Access & Permission System
**Winner:** Human Consequence Map for AI Workflows

Why: generic RBAC is infrastructure. The stronger public question is who can see, decide, override, appeal, or suffer consequences as AI output moves through a workflow. Decision rights and permissions become one layer of a human-consequence graph.

Preserved capability: roles, rights, approval authority, override paths, graph relationships, recourse.

### Replace former 031 — Product Analytics & Telemetry Layer
**Winner:** Evidence Strength & Claim Defensibility Mapper

Why: generic telemetry is Lab plumbing. Evidence defensibility is a recognizable cross-domain problem with exceptional RN fit. Measurement/telemetry still appears as evidence-quality and update signals rather than as the public proposition.

Preserved capability: structured measurement, scoring, event/evidence state, confidence updates, provenance.

### Replace former 033 — Security & Threat Modeling System
**Winner:** Neurotechnology Claim Audit

Why: security remains required Lab practice, but the public slot can carry a far more distinctive scientific/legal/technology question. Threat modeling continues unnumbered and will be explicitly documented in every consequential product.

Preserved public progression: structured risk analysis, claim/evidence decomposition, uncertainty, source provenance, adversarial review. Security primitive itself moves to Lab.

### Replace former 055 — Experience-Optimizing Smart Home Layer
**Winner:** Third-Space / Future-of-Place Decision Lab

Why: the former smart-home concept is technically interesting but narrow and gadget-prone. The decision lab preserves environmental sensing/design reasoning while expanding into workplaces, schools, hospitality, civic spaces, homes, and third spaces through social, sensory, accessibility, economic, environmental, cultural, technological and governance dimensions.

Preserved capability: environmental inputs, multi-criteria decision support, scenario comparison, configurable recommendations, later adaptive-system lineage.

## Reframes retained rather than replaced
The following weak/conditional incumbents remain in the canon only under stronger public titles/constraints:
- 004 → **Human Intelligence Before Automation** — when research-heavy expert work is ready to become a system.
- 006 → **Private Research System → Product / Dataset / Company Decision Engine**.
- 007 → **Governed Learning & Correction Ledger** — how feedback changes a system without erasing history.
- 010 → **Ontology Conflict Mapper** — expose when two institutions use the same word to mean different things.
- 011 → **Entity / Ownership Resolution X-Ray** — aliases, subsidiaries, DBAs, ownership and institutional relationships.
- 019 → **Consequence-Aware Escalation Router** — who must know, when, and with what authority when a system fails.
- 026 → **Regulatory & Research Record Normalizer** — how inconsistent records create false intelligence.
- 032 → **Consequential AI Release Readiness Harness** — what must be demonstrated before release.
- 044 → **Regulated Governance Pattern Library** — reusable patterns with explicit limits on transferability.
- 054 → **Evidence-to-Environment Recommendation Engine** — recommendation synthesis from repeated N-of-1/environment evidence.

## Lab-only primitives now explicitly unnumbered
- authentication/session infrastructure
- generic connectors/ingestion
- generic API gateway/integration plumbing
- deployment/reliability/observability
- generic workflow state-machine implementation
- generic RBAC/permission implementation
- generic product telemetry
- generic security/threat-modeling infrastructure

These are still built and documented in lineage. They simply stop consuming public intellectual slots.

## Result
The candidate canon remains at 100 without adding 101+. All ten Round-2 challengers now have exact public placements. The next task is not more ideation; it is dependency-aware resequencing of the revised titles from least to most technically complex, followed by duplicate/boundary validation of the late regulatory and operating-system clusters.
