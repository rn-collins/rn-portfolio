# Final Admission Records — Builds 051–060

Status: DEEP AUDIT / pre-canon freeze

Controlling standards: general-public comprehension first; expert depth on demand; maximum warranted technical sophistication behind the interface; no public slot exists merely to demonstrate infrastructure; B-Web is the richest warranted interactive explanation; B-LinkedIn is a silent-first 4:5 derivative of the same mechanism.

## 051 — Teach Me the Cannabis Part I Actually Need
**Current incumbent:** Adaptive Cannabis Education System
**Verdict:** KEEP / upgrade.
**Public question:** Why should everyone get the same cannabis lesson when their question, experience, jurisdiction and risk are different?
**Novice path:** Tell the system what you want to understand, what you already know, and your location/context. It builds a short learning path using plain language, misconceptions checks, evidence and jurisdiction-aware caveats.
**Expert depth:** learner modeling, misconception diagnosis, knowledge tracing, evidence grading, jurisdictional policy, adaptive assessment, pedagogical sequencing.
**A technological ceiling:** learner/profile model; knowledge graph; misconception ontology; adaptive sequencing; retrieval from versioned evidence/policy sources; formative assessment; Bayesian/knowledge-tracing style mastery estimates where justified; uncertainty; accessible multimodal explanations; source provenance; jurisdiction/version awareness; privacy-preserving progress storage; evaluation of learning gain, not engagement alone. No personalized medical treatment advice.
**B-Web ceiling:** interactive curriculum graph that physically rearranges when the visitor changes goal, experience, misconception or jurisdiction; knowledge nodes unlock through understanding rather than arbitrary completion.
**B-LinkedIn:** one generic cannabis lesson fractures into radically different paths for different people.
**Saveable payload:** `WHAT DO YOU NEED TO KNOW → WHAT DO YOU ALREADY BELIEVE → WHAT EVIDENCE CHANGES IT → WHAT APPLIES HERE`.
**Sequence contribution:** adaptive learning engine + learner/knowledge graph.

## 052 — Beyond the Strain Name
**Current incumbent:** Beyond-Strain Cannabis Personalization Engine
**Verdict:** KEEP only with strict non-prescriptive boundaries and clear distinction from 049.
**Public question:** If strain names are a weak shortcut, what information is actually useful when comparing cannabis products and contexts?
**Novice path:** compare products/context using composition, format, prior experience, goals and constraints without receiving a treatment/dose prescription.
**Expert depth:** chemotype/composition, product metadata quality, context effects, uncertainty, within-person response, contraindication/safety boundaries, evidence limits.
**A technological ceiling:** structured product/composition normalization; label/OCR import with verification; context/profile integration inherited from 049; explainable similarity/retrieval; uncertainty-aware comparison; rule-based safety/context boundaries; evidence/provenance; personal response updating only from user-controlled records; jurisdiction-aware product metadata where legally/publicly available; no diagnosis, dosing optimization or treatment recommendation.
**B-Web ceiling:** strain label visually collapses while composition/context dimensions expand; visitor changes one variable and watches similarity/relevance reorganize in real time.
**B-LinkedIn:** the famous strain name becomes the smallest data point on screen.
**Saveable payload:** `PRODUCT × COMPOSITION × PERSON × CONTEXT × RESPONSE`.
**Boundary:** 049 learns a person's sensory/context patterns; 052 compares product/context information using those patterns.
**Sequence contribution:** product-normalization + explainable comparison infrastructure.

## 053 — Run a Tiny Experiment on Your Own Environment
**Current incumbent:** N-of-1 Environmental Experimentation Platform
**Verdict:** KEEP / major technical upgrade.
**Public question:** Instead of guessing whether light, sound, layout or nature helps you, can you test one change on yourself carefully enough to learn something useful?
**Novice path:** choose one environmental change and one outcome; the product generates a simple repeated experiment, reminders/observations, and a cautious result.
**Expert depth:** N-of-1 design, randomization/counterbalancing, washout/carryover, repeated measures, adherence, confounding, uncertainty, missingness.
**A technological ceiling:** protocol generator; randomization/counterbalancing engine; time-series capture; optional device/sensor integrations; Bayesian sequential analysis or other appropriate within-person statistics; missingness/adherence handling; context/confound capture; stopping rules; effect/uncertainty visualization; reproducible protocol/export; privacy-first local data option; experiment quality checks. No false causal certainty from weak self-tracking.
**B-Web ceiling:** visitor changes protocol design and sees inferential strength shift; live simulated data demonstrates why one before/after observation is weak and repeated counterbalanced observations are stronger.
**B-LinkedIn:** “I felt better” evolves into an actual N-of-1 experiment.
**Saveable payload:** `CHANGE ONE THING → REPEAT → COMPARE → ACCOUNT FOR CONTEXT → KEEP UNCERTAINTY`.
**Sequence contribution:** personal experiment engine + longitudinal inference primitives.

## 054 — Make the Space Adapt to the Person
**Current incumbent:** Adaptive Environmental Design System
**Verdict:** KEEP / must operationalize 050 + 053 rather than duplicate them.
**Public question:** Can a space change as a person's needs and context change instead of assuming one “optimal” environment?
**Novice path:** choose current activity/need and environmental constraints. Get several explainable configurations and tradeoffs rather than one universal ideal.
**Expert depth:** multi-objective optimization, evidence strength, individual response, accessibility, environmental constraints, adaptation policy, feedback.
**A technological ceiling:** state/context model; evidence rules inherited from 050; personal response model from 053; constraint solver; multi-objective optimization across comfort/focus/accessibility/energy/cost; contextual bandit or adaptive policy only when enough safe feedback exists; uncertainty-aware recommendations; user override; simulation before actuation; explainable recommendations; fairness/accessibility constraints.
**B-Web ceiling:** adaptive room model where activity, sensory need, time and constraints alter the recommended configuration; 2D/3D/WebGL when spatial understanding benefits.
**B-LinkedIn:** the “perfect room” breaks apart because the person and task keep changing.
**Saveable payload:** `PERSON + TASK + CONTEXT + CONSTRAINTS → ADAPTATION, NOT A UNIVERSAL IDEAL`.
**Sequence contribution:** context-aware optimization + adaptation-policy engine.

## 055 — Let the Smart Home Explain Itself
**Current incumbent:** Experience-Optimizing Smart Home Layer
**Verdict:** REFRAME strongly; survives only as an autonomy-first actuation system, not gadget automation.
**Public question:** If a smart environment changes something around you, can you understand why, stop it, and recover when it is wrong?
**Novice path:** connect or simulate a few devices; see proposed changes with reason, confidence and consequence; approve/modify/undo them.
**Expert depth:** device interoperability, local-first control, latency, safety interlocks, permission scopes, automation policy, failure modes, privacy, edge processing.
**A technological ceiling:** standards-aware device abstraction where feasible (e.g. common smart-home protocols/adapters); local-first orchestration; event/state engine; policy/constraint layer; user authority and rollback; safety interlocks; conflict detection between automations; edge inference where beneficial; simulation/digital-twin mode; secure credential handling; audit trail; graceful degradation/offline behavior inherited from 036; explainable actuation from 054 recommendations.
**B-Web ceiling:** interactive home/system digital twin where visitor can let automation act, revoke authority, cause device conflicts, lose network and recover; spatial/3D representation only when useful.
**B-LinkedIn:** “smart” automation acts until the viewer asks the question most smart homes hide: why did it do that, and can I say no?
**Saveable payload:** `RECOMMEND → EXPLAIN → AUTHORIZE → ACT → OVERRIDE → RECOVER`.
**Sequence contribution:** device orchestration + human-authority/rollback layer.

## 056 — What Do You Actually Own That Others Cannot Easily Copy?
**Current incumbent:** Data Moat / Knowledge Moat Mapper
**Verdict:** KEEP / reposition toward founders, creators and institutions of one.
**Public question:** Is your advantage really the data—or is it the relationships, interpretation, workflow, feedback, trust, rights or knowledge around it?
**Novice path:** inventory what a person/company has; system separates commodity inputs from harder-to-replicate assets and explains dependencies/risks.
**Expert depth:** data rights, switching costs, network effects, tacit knowledge, proprietary workflow, feedback loops, IP/trade-secret boundaries, provenance, substitutability.
**A technological ceiling:** asset/relationship knowledge graph; ownership/rights metadata; substitutability and dependency analysis; provenance; competitor/public-availability comparison where lawful; scenario analysis for loss of platform/vendor/team/data; graph centrality; defensibility hypotheses with evidence rather than fake certainty; linkage to Creator Ownership Stack and founder IP systems.
**B-Web ceiling:** interactive moat landscape: remove “proprietary data” and watch whether the advantage survives through relationships, workflow, trust, rights or feedback.
**B-LinkedIn:** a giant DATA MOAT label shrinks while the harder-to-copy system around it appears.
**Saveable payload:** `OWNERSHIP × RIGHTS × RARITY × RELATIONSHIPS × INTERPRETATION × FEEDBACK × SWITCHING COST`.
**Sequence contribution:** defensibility/asset graph.

## 057 — Show Me Where This Content Came From
**Current incumbent:** Content Provenance System
**Verdict:** KEEP / upgrade.
**Public question:** For a finished article, video, image or post, can I see what sources, people, tools and transformations produced it?
**Novice path:** inspect a sample or add a piece of content. See a readable creation history: source material, author/editor actions, AI involvement, approvals, rights and versions.
**Expert depth:** provenance standards, rights/licensing, transformations, model/tool involvement, signatures/attestations, version history, C2PA-like metadata where applicable.
**A technological ceiling:** content lineage graph; file/hash/version metadata; source linking; rights/license registry; transformation events; AI-tool/model disclosure metadata; cryptographic integrity/attestation where useful; support for interoperable provenance standards such as C2PA when media/context warrants; exportable provenance manifest; tamper detection; privacy-sensitive redaction of internal steps.
**B-Web ceiling:** finished artifact can be peeled backward through every source/transformation; graph and timeline stay synchronized; verify integrity states interactively.
**B-LinkedIn:** one polished post grows a visible family tree.
**Saveable payload:** `SOURCE → TRANSFORM → HUMAN/AI ACTION → APPROVAL → RIGHTS → PUBLISH`.
**Sequence contribution:** general content-lineage/provenance infrastructure.

## 058 — Trace a Claim All the Way Back
**Current incumbent:** Source Provenance Infrastructure
**Verdict:** REFRAME from infrastructure into a public Claim Provenance Explorer.
**Public question:** When you see a factual claim, can you get back to the exact evidence it rests on—and know what changed since?
**Novice path:** paste a claim or open a demonstration; follow it to sources, exact supporting passages/records, dates, authority and verification state.
**Expert depth:** claim-source graph, primary/secondary authority, jurisdiction, source versions, excerpt boundaries, contradiction, recency, verification workflow.
**A technological ceiling:** claim extraction; evidence retrieval; source parsing; precise span linkage; provenance graph; source hashing/versioning; authority/primary-source classification; temporal change tracking; contradiction and unsupported-claim detection; citation integrity; human verification queue; exportable claim/evidence package; evaluation against verified claims.
**B-Web ceiling:** literally pull a sentence downward into its evidence roots; sever a source and watch dependent claims lose support; temporal slider shows source updates.
**B-LinkedIn:** a factual sentence looks solid until its roots are revealed—or fail to appear.
**Saveable payload:** `CLAIM → EXACT EVIDENCE → SOURCE → AUTHORITY → DATE/VERSION → VERIFICATION`.
**Boundary:** 057 tracks creation lineage of content objects; 058 tracks epistemic lineage of factual claims.
**Sequence contribution:** claim-source graph + verification infrastructure.

## 059 — Is This Legal Citation Real, Current, and Saying What the AI Claims?
**Current incumbent:** Legal AI Source Provenance & Verification Layer
**Verdict:** KEEP / technologically demanding legal-domain specialization.
**Public question:** An AI gave you a case/statute/regulation and quotation. Can you trust that citation before anyone relies on it?
**Novice path:** paste a citation/AI answer. The product checks whether authority can be located, matches the quoted proposition, applies to the selected jurisdiction/date, and flags what still needs a lawyer/researcher to verify.
**Expert depth:** citation normalization, hierarchy of authority, precedential status, treatment, amendments, jurisdiction, court/date, pin cites, quotations, procedural posture, negative treatment and research completeness.
**A technological ceiling:** legal citation parser/resolver; authoritative-source retrieval/connectors where legally licensed/available; quote-span verification; semantic proposition-to-authority comparison; temporal/statutory versioning; court/jurisdiction ontology; treatment/status metadata; citation graph; hallucination detection; provenance; confidence calibration; human verification workflow. It must never imply that automated verification is a substitute for professional legal research where stakes warrant it.
**B-Web ceiling:** AI answer looks complete; user opens each citation and the system visually tests existence → quotation → proposition → jurisdiction → current status; citation graph changes as bad support is removed.
**B-LinkedIn:** the scariest AI hallucination is the citation that looks perfect.
**Saveable payload:** `EXISTS? → SAYS THAT? → APPLIES HERE? → STILL CURRENT? → TREATED HOW?`.
**Sequence contribution:** legal authority verification specialization over 058.

## 060 — Catch the IP You Create Before It Disappears Into the Workday
**Current incumbent:** Founder Intellectual Property Capture System
**Verdict:** KEEP / strengthen for founders, creators and institutions of one.
**Public question:** What potentially valuable intellectual property are you creating every day without recognizing, documenting or routing it?
**Novice path:** describe/import a work artifact or recurring output. Product helps identify possible categories—brand, invention, creative work, method/process, dataset, confidential know-how—then asks what should be documented, kept confidential, reviewed professionally or simply ignored.
**Expert depth:** copyright/trademark/patent/trade-secret distinctions, ownership, assignments, inventorship/authorship, disclosure timing, confidentiality, provenance, evidence of creation, jurisdictional nuance.
**A technological ceiling:** multimodal artifact intake; entity/IP candidate extraction; provenance/time-stamping; rights/ownership graph; confidentiality and disclosure-state tracking; duplicate/related-work detection; decision routing; version history; optional integrations with source repositories/content systems; secure local/private storage; exportable evidence package; rule-based jurisdiction/context prompts with sourced legal information. Never automatically declare legal ownership, patentability, registrability or protection.
**B-Web ceiling:** a normal workday emits documents, code, names, methods, datasets and ideas; visitor catches/routs them into an evolving IP map while uncaptured items visibly disappear or become public.
**B-LinkedIn:** “my IP” stops meaning one patent and becomes the stream of assets leaking out of ordinary work.
**Saveable payload:** `NOTICE → CLASSIFY → PROVE CREATION → CHECK OWNERSHIP → CONTROL DISCLOSURE → DECIDE`.
**Sequence contribution:** founder asset/provenance/IP graph feeding later ownership systems.
