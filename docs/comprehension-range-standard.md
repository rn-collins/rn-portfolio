# Comprehension Range Standard

## Why this replaces “education range”
Formal education is an imperfect proxy for whether someone can understand or use a product. A person without a degree may have deep practical expertise; a highly educated person may be new to the domain; age, disability, language, attention, context, and familiarity also affect comprehension.

The controlling requirement is therefore **comprehension range**, not educational attainment.

## Public rule
**General-public by default. Expert depth on demand.**

Do not show visitors labels such as middle-school, college, or expert version. Do not create separate patronizing editions unless a real use case independently warrants one.

## Internal test bands
Test the same primary product against different kinds of users/context:
1. younger or early-secondary reader with no domain knowledge;
2. general adult with no specialist training;
3. non-specialist with strong general literacy;
4. practitioner/professional in an adjacent field;
5. domain professional;
6. technical/domain expert.

These are QA lenses, not audience identities.

## Required outcome
All six should be able to identify the same core purpose and core result. The difference is how much optional depth each person needs or wants.

The primary path must answer in ordinary language:
- What is this?
- Is this relevant to me?
- What do I do?
- What will I get?
- What does my result mean?
- What can I do next?

Advanced layers may expose:
- exact terminology;
- methodology;
- evidence and provenance;
- assumptions and uncertainty;
- configuration;
- technical architecture;
- domain-specific nuance;
- export/integration controls.

## Failure conditions
A build fails if:
- understanding the first screen requires domain vocabulary;
- the user must understand the technology underneath before receiving value;
- simplifying copy destroys material accuracy;
- expert detail contaminates the novice path instead of using progressive disclosure;
- the interface infantilizes general users;
- the same core meaning changes across comprehension bands;
- accessibility is treated as synonymous with low education.

## Copy rule
Concrete action before abstraction. Familiar words before specialist labels. When a specialist term matters, explain it in context and preserve the precise term in deeper detail.
