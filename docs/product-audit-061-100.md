## Final product-level finding
The late sequence is allowed to become extremely sophisticated technically, but the public interaction must become *more legible*, not more expert-dependent. Build 100 may contain simulation, optimization and graph infrastructure while still opening with an understandable scenario question. Technical complexity is evidence of craft; it is not a tax imposed on the visitor.
