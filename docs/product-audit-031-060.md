## Cross-batch finding
The strongest middle builds hide increasingly advanced evidence, graph, geospatial, evaluation and personalization infrastructure behind one obvious task. No public product should ask a novice to configure embeddings, ontologies, agents, APIs, graph schemas, model providers or evaluation harnesses to obtain first value.
